**The best Bible verses for lust are short enough to say out loud in the moment and clear enough to move your feet: 1 Corinthians 10:13, 2 Timothy 2:22, 1 Corinthians 6:18-20, James 1:14-15, Job 31:1, Romans 13:14, Philippians 4:8 and Galatians 5:16. Learn one before you need it, say it out loud when the pull comes, and leave the room while you say it.** A verse is not a magic word. It is the truth, spoken while you act on it.

Most lists of Bible verses about lust stop at the list. You read twenty verses, feel convicted, and then at 11 p.m. you cannot remember a single one. So this article does it differently. Each verse comes with what it means, when to use it, and what to do with your body while you say it.

## How do Bible verses help with lust?

Watch what Jesus did when He was tempted in the wilderness. He did not argue with the devil or try to out-think him. He answered with Scripture.

> **[Matthew 4:4 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+4:4&version=NKJV)** "But He answered and said, 'It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceeds from the mouth of God.'"

It is written. Temptation is a voice telling you something false: you need this, it will help, no one will know, it does not matter. A verse is a second voice in the room, telling you what is true. When you say it out loud, the lie stops being the only thing you hear.

But notice that Jesus also moved. He did not stay in conversation. The verse and the movement go together.

> **[Psalm 119:11 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+119:11&version=NKJV)** "Your word I have hidden in my heart, That I might not sin against You."

Hidden in my heart means learned ahead of time. You cannot pull out what you never put in.

## Is lust a sin or just a temptation?

This matters, because a lot of people feel guilty for being tempted and then give up because they feel they already sinned.

Temptation is not sin. Jesus was tempted in every way as we are, yet without sin (Hebrews 4:15). The thought that flashes into your mind, the image that crosses your screen, the pull you feel, none of that is sin yet.

> **[James 1:14-15 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=James+1:14-15&version=NKJV)** "But each one is tempted when he is drawn away by his own desires and enticed. Then, when desire has conceived, it gives birth to sin; and sin, when it is full-grown, brings forth death."

James lays out a chain. Desire. Drawn away. Enticed. Conceived. Sin. Death. Each step is a place where you can stop.

Here is how to use it. In the moment, name where you are. "I am being enticed. Nothing has been conceived yet. I can still walk away." Naming the step turns a vague pull into a clear choice. The earlier in the chain you act, the easier it is.

There is something even earlier than the chain. Before you choose between good and evil, you choose who you will listen to. Decide in the morning, before the pull comes, that today you will be led by your heavenly Father. That settles a lot of the later choices.

## What Bible verse says lust is a sin?

> **[Matthew 5:28 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+5:28&version=NKJV)** "But I say to you that whoever looks at a woman to lust for her has already committed adultery with her in his heart."

Jesus moved the line from the body to the heart. Lust is not only an action. It is a way of looking at a person as something to use. That applies to women looking at men too.

Use this verse in the morning, not in the moment. It is a verse for setting your standard, not for beating yourself up at midnight. If you have already fallen, go to [Does God Still Love Me After Porn?](/teachings/does-god-still-love-me-after-porn) and come back here later.

## What do I say when the pull comes?

These are the verses for the moment itself. Learn at least one.

**1 Corinthians 10:13. The way out.**

> **[1 Corinthians 10:13 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Corinthians+10:13&version=NKJV)** "No temptation has overtaken you except such as is common to man; but God is faithful, who will not allow you to be tempted beyond what you are able, but with the temptation will also make the way of escape, that you may be able to bear it."

Two truths. You are not uniquely broken, because this is common. And there is a way of escape built into every temptation. When the pull is loud, look for it. It is almost always small and close. The door. The light switch. The phone in the next room. The friend you can text.

**1 Corinthians 6:18. Flee.**

> **[1 Corinthians 6:18 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Corinthians+6:18-20&version=NKJV)** "Flee sexual immorality..."

Flee is a feet word. Paul uses fight language for many things in his letters, but for sexual temptation he says run. Do not negotiate. Do not see how close you can get. Stand up and leave the room while you say it.

**2 Timothy 2:22. Run toward something.**

> **[2 Timothy 2:22 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Timothy+2:22&version=NKJV)** "Flee also youthful lusts; but pursue righteousness, faith, love, peace with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart."

You do not only run away. You run toward. Toward people, too: "with those who call on the Lord." That is why a text to your accountability partner is a form of obedience to this verse. If you do not have one, read [How to Find a Christian Accountability Partner](/teachings/christian-accountability-partner).

## Which verses do I use before temptation comes?

Most of the battle is won before the moment. These verses are for setting your day.

**Job 31:1. Decide in advance.**

> **[Job 31:1 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Job+31:1&version=NKJV)** "I have made a covenant with my eyes; Why then should I look upon a young woman?"

Job did not wait for the moment to decide. He made a covenant ahead of time. Say this one in the morning before you open your phone.

**Romans 13:14. Remove the supply.**

> **[Romans 13:14 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+13:14&version=NKJV)** "But put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh, to fulfill its lusts."

Make no provision. This is the verse behind every filter, every phone that charges outside the bedroom, every app you delete. Read [Accountability Apps Compared](/teachings/accountability-apps-compared-2026) and [How to Stop Scrolling at Night](/teachings/stop-scrolling-at-night-christian) to put it into practice.

**Psalm 119:9. Keep the way clean.**

> **[Psalm 119:9 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+119:9&version=NKJV)** "How can a young man cleanse his way? By taking heed according to Your word."

Taking heed is daily. A few minutes in Scripture each morning does more than an hour of panic at night. The lesson [A Few Minutes With Scripture](/tracks/discipline-and-morning-routine/a-few-minutes-with-scripture) shows how to build that habit.

## What do I think about instead?

You cannot just empty your mind. An empty room does not stay empty. You need something to put in its place.

> **[Philippians 4:8 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Philippians+4:8&version=NKJV)** "Finally, brethren, whatever things are true, whatever things are noble, whatever things are just, whatever things are pure, whatever things are lovely, whatever things are of good report, if there is any virtue and if there is anything praiseworthy, meditate on these things."

Use this one right after you have left the room. Pick one true and good thing and think about it on purpose. A person you love. Something your heavenly Father did for you this year. A plan for tomorrow. For the full practice of catching a thought and replacing it, read [Take Every Thought Captive](/teachings/take-every-thought-captive-2026).

## Can a verse actually kill lust?

No verse works like a spell. If you say 1 Corinthians 10:13 while you keep scrolling, it will not save you.

> **[Galatians 5:16 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5:16&version=NKJV)** "I say then: Walk in the Spirit, and you shall not fulfill the lust of the flesh."

The real answer is a relationship, not a technique. Walk in the Spirit. Verses help you walk with Him. He is the one who actually changes the desire over time. That is why the fight against lust starts with knowing who you are in Christ. You are not a lustful person trying to act holy. You are a child of your heavenly Father learning to live like one. The [Know Who You Are](/tracks/know-who-you-are) course is built on that.

> **[Romans 6:14 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+6:14&version=NKJV)** "For sin shall not have dominion over you, for you are not under law but under grace."

Grace is not weaker than the law. It is stronger. Read [What Is Grace?](/teachings/what-is-grace-2026) if that sounds backward.

## Start with one verse this week

Do not try to memorize all of these tonight. Pick one. 1 Corinthians 10:13 is a good start. Say it every morning for seven days. By the end of the week it will be there when you need it.
