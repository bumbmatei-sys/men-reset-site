**When you keep falling into the same sin, pray quickly, honestly and simply, as a child talking to a Father: "Father, I did it again. It was sin. Thank You that You forgive me. Clean my heart and help me." You do not need a longer prayer, a better performance, or a waiting period. You need to come.** Then pray for the root, not only the record, and take one practical step with Him.

If you are tired of saying the same prayer, I understand. Maybe you have stopped praying about it at all because you are embarrassed. "Again, Lord?" It feels like you have used up your chances.

You have not. Let me show you why, and then give you a way to pray that does not depend on how well you did this week.

## Why does praying feel so hard after I fall again?

Because you think prayer is a performance, and you just failed the audition.

Shame turns prayer into a courtroom. You walk in as the defendant, ready to explain, promise, and try to sound sorry enough. After the tenth time, you run out of explanations and promises. So you stop going in.

But your heavenly Father is not a judge waiting for your best defense. He is a Father waiting for His child. When the prodigal son came home, he had rehearsed a speech about becoming a hired servant.

> **[Luke 15:21-22 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+15:21-22&version=NKJV)** "And the son said to him, 'Father, I have sinned against heaven and in your sight, and am no longer worthy to be called your son.' But the father said to his servants, 'Bring out the best robe and put it on him, and put a ring on his hand and sandals on his feet.'"

The father did not let him finish the servant part. He did not argue with the speech. He just answered it with a robe and a ring. Prayer after failure is not a speech you have to get right. It is coming home.

## Will God get tired of forgiving me?

This is the real fear. Not "Will He forgive me this time?" but "How many times until He stops?"

Look at what Jesus told Peter.

> **[Matthew 18:21-22 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+18:21-22&version=NKJV)** "Then Peter came to Him and said, 'Lord, how often shall my brother sin against me, and I forgive him? Up to seven times?' Jesus said to him, 'I do not say to you, up to seven times, but up to seventy times seven.'"

And in Luke, Jesus said if your brother sins against you seven times in one day and comes back seven times saying "I repent," you forgive him (Luke 17:4). Seven times in one day. For the same thing, most likely.

Here is the point. If Jesus asks that of us, ordinary people with small hearts, His own mercy is not smaller than what He asks of us. Your heavenly Father is not keeping a count that runs out.

> **[Lamentations 3:22-23 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Lamentations+3:22-23&version=NKJV)** "Through the Lord's mercies we are not consumed, Because His compassions fail not. They are new every morning; Great is Your faithfulness."

Fail not. New every morning. There is no daily limit.

## What does Jesus do with someone who fails Him again?

Peter is the best picture. He promised Jesus he would never deny Him. Hours later, he denied Him three times. Then Jesus was crucified, and Peter had to live with that.

After the resurrection, Jesus found Peter on a beach. He did not ask, "Why did you fail?" He did not make Peter promise to do better. He asked one question three times.

> **[John 21:17 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+21:17&version=NKJV)** "He said to him the third time, 'Simon, son of Jonah, do you love Me?' Peter was grieved because He said to him the third time, 'Do you love Me?' And he said to Him, 'Lord, You know all things; You know that I love You.' Jesus said to him, 'Feed My sheep.'"

Three denials, three chances to say "I love You." And then a calling. Jesus restored Peter and gave him work to do. He did not put him on probation.

That is how He prays with you after a fall. The question is not "How bad was it?" The question is "Do you love Me?" You can answer that honestly even on your worst night.

## How do I pray when I keep falling?

Here is a simple 4-part prayer. It takes two minutes. Pray it out loud if you can.

| Part | What you do | What you can say |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Father | Start with who He is to you. Not "Lord, I am so terrible." Start with relationship. | "Father, I am Your child. I am coming home." |
| 2. The truth | Say plainly what you did. No excuses, no long speech, no graphic detail. | "I did it again. I watched porn. It was sin." |
| 3. Your word | Agree with what He has said, not with what shame says. Read it if you need to. | "You said if I confess, You are faithful and just to forgive me and cleanse me. I receive that now. Thank You." |
| 4. Help | Ask for the root, not only the record, and ask for one step. | "Clean my heart. Show me why I keep going there. Help me with the next step." |

Notice that part four asks for more than forgiveness. That is on purpose.

> **[1 John 1:9 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+John+1:9&version=NKJV)** "If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness."

There are two promises in that verse. He forgives the sins, the things you did. And He cleanses the unrighteousness, the thing in you that keeps going back. A habit often needs both. If you only ever pray for forgiveness, you are asking Him to clean up the spill but not fix the leak.

> **[Psalm 51:10 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+51:10&version=NKJV)** "Create in me a clean heart, O God, And renew a steadfast spirit within me."

David prayed this after one of the worst falls in the Bible. Make it yours.

## Why do I keep falling into the same sin?

Ask Him. Seriously. Most repeated sin has a root you have not looked at.

> **[Psalm 139:23-24 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+139:23-24&version=NKJV)** "Search me, O God, and know my heart; Try me, and know my anxieties; And see if there is any wicked way in me, And lead me in the way everlasting."

Know my anxieties. David connected his sin with what he was anxious about. Often the sin you keep falling into is trying to meet a real need in a false way. Loneliness. Stress. Boredom. Feeling like a failure. The urge is the symptom. The need is the root.

So when you pray, ask, "Father, what was I really looking for tonight?" Then listen. Write down what comes. Bring that need to Him directly. And close the practical door too, because prayer and action go together. [What to Do After a Porn Relapse](/teachings/what-to-do-after-porn-relapse) shows how to find the door that was open.

And do not do this alone. Most people who keep falling are fighting alone. Read [How to Find a Christian Accountability Partner](/teachings/christian-accountability-partner).

## What if I have no words left?

Then you do not need them.

> **[Romans 8:26 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+8:26&version=NKJV)** "Likewise the Spirit also helps in our weaknesses. For we do not know what we should pray for as we ought, but the Spirit Himself makes intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered."

When you are too tired, too ashamed, or too empty to pray well, the Spirit prays for you. Your job is just to come.

When I was a teenager, I was empty, angry and depressed, and for a long time I saw no reason to live. The prayer that changed everything for me was not long or theological. It was one sentence from a broken heart: "Jesus, if You really exist, help me." He answered. A short honest prayer is not a lesser prayer. Sometimes it is the most real one you will ever pray.

So if all you can say is "Father, help me," say it. That is enough.

## How do I keep praying when nothing seems to change?

Keep a place with Him that you visit every day, not only after you fall.

There is a place in your heavenly Father that only you can fill. A spot carved out for you. When you only go there after failure, you start to think of prayer as an emergency room. But it is meant to be home. Visit it in the morning when nothing has gone wrong yet. Do not let weeds grow there.

Start with a few minutes each morning. He began it. He will finish it. Your part is to keep coming.
