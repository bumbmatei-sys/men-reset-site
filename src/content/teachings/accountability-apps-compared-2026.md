Then, if you want more, pick one paid app that matches what you actually need: blocking, screenshots, or plain text reports. "An app is a fence, not a Savior." Jesus is the Savior. You searched for the best accountability app because you want this to be over. Maybe you have tried willpower. Maybe you have tried deleting browsers and then re-installing them at midnight. Here is the honest truth. The brand of app matters less than two other things: walking in the light, and telling one trusted person the truth. Same sex, one person, real names, real conversations. That is where freedom grows.

## What is the free porn blocker on iPhone (and Android)?

Let's start with free, because you should not pay first.

On iPhone and iPad, go to Settings, then Screen Time, then Content & Privacy Restrictions, then Content Restrictions, then Web Content. Choose "Limit Adult Websites." You can also add specific sites to "Never Allow." Ask someone you trust to set the Screen Time passcode for you. If you know the passcode, you will change it back on a hard night. That is not a character flaw. That is just how hard nights work.

Apple also offers Communication Safety in Messages. It blurs sensitive images before a younger person sees them. Read the details on the Apple Support site so you understand what it covers and what it does not.

On Android, Google Family Link lets a supervising adult turn on explicit site controls for a supervised account. Google Digital Wellbeing gives you app timers and Focus mode. Those help with habits. On its own, Digital Wellbeing is not a porn filter. It is a timer. Use it alongside Family Link, or alongside one of the paid tools below.

One more free option. Most routers and many DNS providers let you filter adult sites for your whole home. This is helpful. It is also easy to switch back to mobile data. Treat it as one more fence post, not the whole fence.

## What do accountability apps actually do?

There are three different jobs here. People mix them up, so they buy the wrong thing.

**Blocking.** The app tries to stop adult content before you see it. Covenant Eyes and Canopy both block. Ever Accountable has an optional filter. Accountable2You does not block by default.

**Screenshots.** The app captures what is on the screen and sends blurred or filtered images to your partner. Covenant Eyes and Ever Accountable do this. Accountable2You does not.

**Text reports and alerts.** The app sends a summary of activity, with flags when something looks risky. Accountable2You is built around fast, plain text reports and quick alerts. Covenant Eyes and Canopy also report.

Here is the thing no vendor tells you loudly. A fence is not a person. Software cannot confess, cannot pray with you, cannot put a hand on your shoulder and say "I am still here." That is what James 5:16 is for.

> **[James 5:16 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=James+5:16&version=NKJV)** "Confess your trespasses to one another, and pray for one another, that you may be healed." The app helps you live that out. It does not replace it.

## Covenant Eyes vs Accountable2You: which one?

This is the comparison most people want, so let me be direct.

Pick Covenant Eyes if you want blocking built in, and you want your partner to see blurred screenshots. Screenshots feel heavy to some people. To others, they are the one thing that actually stops them at 1 a.m. The annual price is $198. There is also a $950 lifetime option if you plan to use it for years.

Pick Accountable2You if you want fast text reports and alerts without screenshots. The Personal plan is $11/mo or $121/yr and covers 6 installs. Family is $16/mo or $176/yr for 20 installs. The trade-off is real: it does not block by default. You add your own fence through Screen Time, Family Link, or DNS filtering, and Accountable2You tells the truth about what happened.

Neither one saves you. Both give you a gatekeeper who is not your own willpower at midnight. [Proverbs 27:17 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs+27:17&version=NKJV) says, "As iron sharpens iron, so a man sharpens the countenance of his friend." The app is just the bell on the gate. Your friend is the iron.

## Which accountability app is right for which man (or woman)?

This is for men and for women. Porn is not a men's-only struggle, and the same freedom in Christ is for you. Pick a same-sex accountability partner. That keeps the relationship clean and honest for everyone.

If you are a student or on a tight budget: start with Screen Time or Family Link, and add Accountable2You Personal when you can. Ask your church if it helps with the cost. Many do, quietly.

If you are married and your spouse is part of your recovery: Canopy's filtering on multiple devices helps a household. Keep the deep honesty with a same-sex friend. Your spouse needs a partner, not a parole officer.

If you have young people in the home: Canopy Plus or Premium, or Covenant Eyes with its multi-user coverage, makes sense. Talk to your kids in plain language. Shame drives hiding. Honesty invites help.

If you keep relapsing through work devices or smart TVs: Ever Accountable's unlimited devices for one reporting user is worth a look, so is Covenant Eyes with up to 10 users.

If screenshots feel like too much: Accountable2You. Full stop. Go with text reports and pair it with a free blocker.

## What privacy questions should you ask before you install?

Good. A little caution is wise, not fearful. Ask these out loud.

Who sees my reports? One person, or a whole group? Start with one.

What exactly is captured? Full screenshots, blurred screenshots, domain lists, app names?

Where is the data stored, and for how long? Check the vendor's privacy page.

Can my partner see other people in the house? If you have family on the plan, know the boundaries before you install.

What happens if I want to uninstall? Most apps notify your partner. That is a feature. Tell yourself now that the notification is the point.

Write the answers down together with your partner. Ephesians 5:11-13 is clear.

> **[Ephesians 5:11-13 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+5:11-13&version=NKJV)** "And have no fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather expose them. For it is shameful even to speak of those things which are done by them in secret. But all things that are exposed are made manifest by the light, for whatever makes manifest is light."

Exposing them is not about humiliation. It is about bringing things into the light where they lose their power.

## How do I set one up today?

Do not wait for a perfect plan. Do this in the next hour.

1. **Start free first.** Set Screen Time "Limit Adult Websites" on iPhone, or Family Link explicit site controls on Android. Ask someone else to hold the passcode.

2. **Or pick one paid app.** Choose based on what you need: blocking, screenshots, or text reports. Most have a trial. Use the trial fully.

3. **Invite one trusted person.** Same sex. Someone who loves Jesus and loves you enough to ask hard questions. Send the invite tonight, not next week.

4. **Put it on every device.** Phone, tablet, laptop, work computer if it is yours, the TV browser. One gap is all a hard night needs.

5. **Decide now what happens when the alert comes.** Agree on it before it fires. For example: call within one hour, then meet in person within two days. No lectures. No drama. Just honesty and prayer.

6. **Pray James 5:16 style.** Confess to each other. Pray for each other. Ask your heavenly Father for healing. Romans 8:1 is your anchor.

> **[Romans 8:1 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+8:1&version=NKJV)** "There is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus." Confession positions your heart back on Him. You are not earning forgiveness again. You already have it.
