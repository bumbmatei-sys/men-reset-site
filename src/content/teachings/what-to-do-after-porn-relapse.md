**After a porn relapse, do four things: stop the spiral in the first ten minutes, come back to your heavenly Father the same hour, tell one trusted person within a day, and close the one door that was open. Then keep a small seven-day plan instead of making a giant vow.** One fall does not have to become a week. You can turn around right now.

If you are reading this a few minutes after it happened, good. You are already doing the most important thing. You are not hiding.

This is not a lecture. You know it was wrong. You feel it. So let us skip the scolding and go straight to what to do next.

## What do I do in the first ten minutes?

Stop the spiral. That is the only job of the first ten minutes.

The most dangerous moment after a relapse is not the relapse. It is the thought that comes right after: "I already blew it, so it does not matter now." That thought turns one fall into a whole night, and one night into a lost week.

Answer it out loud. "One fall does not require a second." Then move.

1. Close everything. Every tab, every app, every private window. Delete anything you saved. Do not scroll back through your history to measure how bad it was.
2. Leave the room. Put the phone in another room. If it is night, it charges outside your bedroom from now on.
3. Turn on a light. Wash your face. Drink a glass of water.
4. Move your body. Walk around the block, do twenty push-ups, step outside for a minute.

If it is late, the best thing you can do after this is go to sleep. Most of the next steps can wait until morning. The spiral cannot.

> **[Micah 7:8 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Micah+7:8&version=NKJV)** "Do not rejoice over me, my enemy; When I fall, I will arise; When I sit in darkness, The Lord will be a light to me."

When I fall, I will arise. Not if. The prophet expected to fall sometimes, and he expected to get up.

## How do I come back to God after a relapse?

The same hour. You do not need a cooling-off period. Shame will tell you to stay away from prayer for a while because you are too dirty. That is a lie, and it is the lie that sets up the next fall.

Say it to your heavenly Father plainly. "Father, I watched porn. It was sin. I am sorry. Thank You that You forgive me." Then read this out loud and take Him at His word.

> **[1 John 1:9 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+John+1:9&version=NKJV)** "If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness."

Faithful means He does what He says, every time, including this time. And notice the second promise. He does not only forgive the act. He cleanses the unrighteousness underneath it. Ask Him to do that. "Clean the part of me that went looking."

If you are not sure He still wants you, read [Does God Still Love Me After Porn?](/teachings/does-god-still-love-me-after-porn). The short answer is yes.

## Does a relapse mean I have to start over?

Your day counter may restart. Your life does not.

If you had thirty clean days and then fell, you did not lose those thirty days. You learned to go thirty days. You built habits, you closed doors, you prayed. All of that is still yours.

> **[Proverbs 24:16 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs+24:16&version=NKJV)** "For a righteous man may fall seven times And rise again..."

The righteous man in this verse falls. What makes him righteous is not that he never falls. It is that he rises again. Your identity is not your streak. You are a son or a daughter of your heavenly Father, and that did not change tonight. The [Know Who You Are](/tracks/know-who-you-are) course is built on this.

Grace does not reset with your counter. That is why it is called grace.

## Who should I tell, and when?

Tell one trusted person of the same sex within a day. Your accountability partner, a pastor, a mature friend.

Secrecy is where the next relapse gets planned. Every hour you keep it hidden, shame gets another hour to work. A short message is enough:

"I slipped last night. Can we talk today?"

They do not need graphic details. They need the truth, early. Let them pray for you. Let them ask questions. If you do not have this person yet, that is the real gap. Read [How to Find a Christian Accountability Partner](/teachings/christian-accountability-partner) this week.

If you are married, decide with your partner or counselor how and when you tell your wife. Hiding it from her rebuilds the wall you are trying to tear down. [How to Tell Your Wife You Watched Porn](/teachings/how-to-tell-your-wife-about-porn) walks through that with care.

## Why did I relapse?

Most relapses are not mysteries. They follow a pattern long before the click. Once you are calm, ideally the next day, write down four answers.

> **[Lamentations 3:40 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Lamentations+3:40&version=NKJV)** "Let us search out and examine our ways, And turn back to the Lord;"

Examine and turn back. Those go together. Here is what to examine:

- **When?** The time, the day, the place. Late at night in bed? Sunday afternoon alone?
- **What was I feeling an hour before?** Tired, lonely, stressed, bored, angry, rejected?
- **How did I get access?** Which device, which app, which workaround. Be exact. Vague answers make vague fixes.
- **What did I skip?** Sleep, a meal, prayer, the gym, the friend who usually checks in?

You will usually find the answer in one of those four. The [Break Free](/tracks/break-free) lesson [Why Do I Keep Going Back?](/tracks/break-free/why-you-keep-going-back-to-porn) goes deeper into the loop.

## What should I change after a relapse?

Change one door and one rhythm. Skip the giant vow.

After a fall, the instinct is to swing big. Never again. Delete every app. Fast for forty days. Those vows feel holy at midnight and collapse by Thursday, and then you have one more thing to feel ashamed about.

Instead, pick one door to close and one rhythm to add.

A door limits access. The phone charges outside the bedroom. The browser that got around your filter gets removed. Your filter gets a setup that needs your partner to turn it off.

A rhythm changes the life around the fall. A set bedtime. A midweek call with your accountability partner. Ten minutes in Scripture before you look at your phone in the morning. The [Discipline and a Morning Routine](/tracks/discipline-and-morning-routine) course builds that one step at a time, and [The Phone Sleeps in Another Room](/tracks/discipline-and-morning-routine/the-phone-sleeps-in-another-room) is the single most useful door for most people.

> **[Romans 13:14 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+13:14&version=NKJV)** "But put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh, to fulfill its lusts."

Make no provision. That is what closing a door is. You are not relying on willpower at 11 p.m. You are removing the provision at 3 p.m.

## What if I keep relapsing?

Then you keep coming back, and you get more help.

Measure your comeback, not only your streak. How long did it take you to come back to your Father after this fall? An hour? A day? A week? If that gap is getting shorter, you are winning something real.

If falls keep coming, add more support. A weekly call instead of a monthly one. A Christian counselor who works with sexual integrity. A recovery group at a local church. None of that is failure. It is wisdom. If you also feel hopeless or numb most days, talk to a doctor as well.

And when you keep falling, pray differently, not less. [How to Pray When You Keep Falling](/teachings/how-to-pray-when-you-keep-falling) shows how.

Not tomorrow. Now.

Close the screen. Say it to your heavenly Father. Read 1 John 1:9 out loud. Text your person in the morning. Close one door. Check the first box.
