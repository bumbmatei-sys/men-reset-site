**What kind of father is God? He is the Father Jesus shows you. He loves you before you do anything, sees you from far away and runs toward you, knows what you need, gives good gifts, is tender and patient, corrects you to restore you, and rejoices over you. If your picture of God looks more like a cold judge or a tired, disappointed dad, the answer is not to try harder. It is to look at Jesus.**

Many of us carry a picture of God around without ever looking at it. When you fail, what face do you imagine He is making? When you pray, do you expect Him to lean in or to sigh?

For years I believed in God without knowing Him as a Father. I grew up around church, but inside I was angry, lonely and empty. One night, out of a broken heart, I prayed a short prayer: "Jesus, if You really exist, help me." What filled me in that moment was not a lecture. It was love, warm and real, unlike anything I had felt before. I was a nobody, and He made me His beloved son.

That is the Father I want to show you here. Not from my feelings, but from the Bible, and especially from Jesus.

## What kind of father is God?

Jesus answered this question directly. One of His disciples, Philip, asked Him to show them the Father. Jesus said:

> **[John 14:9 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+14:9&version=NKJV)** "He who has seen Me has seen the Father; so how can you say, 'Show us the Father'?"

So if you want to understand the heavenly Father, watch what Jesus does. Jesus said He did only what He saw the Father do ([John 5:19](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+5:19&version=NKJV)). When He touches a leper, that is the Father. When He welcomes children, that is the Father. When He forgives the woman everyone else wanted to stone, that is the Father.

Whatever you believe about God that does not look like Jesus needs to be questioned.

## Why is God called Father?

In the Old Testament, God is called Father only a few times. Then Jesus comes and calls Him Father again and again, in almost every prayer we have from Him. And He tells us to do the same:

> **[Matthew 6:9 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+6:9&version=NKJV)** "In this manner, therefore, pray: Our Father in heaven, Hallowed be Your name."

Notice the order. Before "hallowed be Your name," before any request, comes "Our Father." Holiness and closeness are not enemies. He is holy, and He is yours.

## 7 things Jesus shows you about the heavenly Father

### 1. He loves you before you do anything

At Jesus' baptism, before He had preached a sermon or healed anyone, a voice came from heaven:

> **[Matthew 3:17 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+3:17&version=NKJV)** "This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased."

The Father's voice is a voice of affirmation. It comes first, not as a reward at the end. And because you are in Christ, that same favor rests on you. You do not have to strive or perform to prove your worth to Him. The lesson [Loved Before You Do Anything](/tracks/know-gods-love/loved-before-you-do-anything) goes deeper into this.

### 2. He runs toward you

In the story of the lost son, the son comes home rehearsing a speech. But the father does not wait for it:

> **[Luke 15:20 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+15:20&version=NKJV)** "But when he was still a great way off, his father saw him and had compassion, and ran and fell on his neck and kissed him."

He saw him "a great way off." That means he was watching the road. Jesus tells this story to show you the Father's heart. He is not waiting for you to clean yourself up. He is looking for you.

### 3. He knows what you need

> **[Matthew 6:8 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+6:8&version=NKJV)** "For your Father knows the things you have need of before you ask Him."

Prayer is not convincing a reluctant God to care. He already cares. Prayer is a child talking to a Father who is already paying attention.

### 4. He gives good gifts

> **[Matthew 7:11 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+7:9-11&version=NKJV)** "If you then, being evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father who is in heaven give good things to those who ask Him!"

Jesus is saying: think of the best moment you have seen a father give something to his child. The heavenly Father is "how much more" than that. [James 1:17](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=James+1:17&version=NKJV) adds that every good gift comes from "the Father of lights, with whom there is no variation or shadow of turning." He does not have good days and bad days with you.

### 5. He is tender and patient

> **[Psalm 103:13 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+103:13&version=NKJV)** "As a father pities his children, So the Lord pities those who fear Him."

Through the prophet Hosea, God describes Himself teaching His people to walk, holding them by the arms, drawing them "with gentle cords, With bands of love," and stooping down to feed them ([Hosea 11:3-4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Hosea+11:3-4&version=NKJV)). That is the picture of a father bending down to a small child who keeps falling while learning to walk. He is not shocked that you fall. He is teaching you to walk.

### 6. He corrects you to restore you, not to destroy you

A good father does correct his children. But look at why the heavenly Father does it:

> **[Hebrews 12:10 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Hebrews+12:10&version=NKJV)** "For they indeed for a few days chastened us as seemed best to them, but He for our profit, that we may be partakers of His holiness."

Earthly fathers sometimes correct out of anger, stress or embarrassment. The heavenly Father corrects "for our profit." His correction always moves you closer to Him, never away. If what you hear in your head only pushes you into shame and hiding, that is not the Father's voice.

> **[1 John 4:18 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+John+4:18&version=NKJV)** "There is no fear in love; but perfect love casts out fear, because fear involves torment."

### 7. He rejoices over you

> **[Zephaniah 3:17 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Zephaniah+3:17&version=NKJV)** "He will rejoice over you with gladness, He will quiet you with His love, He will rejoice over you with singing."

And on the night before the cross, Jesus prayed that the world would know the Father "loved them as You have loved Me" ([John 17:23](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+17:23&version=NKJV)). Read that slowly. The Father loves you the way He loves Jesus. Not a smaller, leftover love.

## Is God a father to everyone?

This is an honest question, and it deserves an honest answer. God made every person, and He loves the whole world. [John 3:16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+3:16&version=NKJV) says He gave His Son because He "so loved the world."

At the same time, the Bible says we become His children by receiving Jesus:

> **[John 1:12 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+1:12&version=NKJV)** "But as many as received Him, to them He gave the right to become children of God, to those who believe in His name."

So the invitation is for everyone. No one is too far, too broken or too late. The door into the family is Jesus. If you have never come to Him, you can do it today, the same way I did: honestly, in your own words.

## What if your earthly father was absent or harsh?

Most of us do not start with a blank picture of God. We start with the face of the man who raised us, or the empty space where he should have been. If your dad was cold, you may expect God to be cold. If he was unpredictable, you may never feel safe with God. If he was proud of you only when you performed, you may feel you have to perform for God too.

The heavenly Father is not a bigger version of your earthly father. Jesus shows you what He is really like.

> **[Psalm 68:5 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+68:5&version=NKJV)** "A father of the fatherless, a defender of widows, Is God in His holy habitation."

You do not have to pretend your father did not hurt you. You can be honest about it and still receive what was missing. If this is a deep wound for you, the lesson [The Father You Had, and the Father You Have](/tracks/breaking-the-family-pattern/the-father-you-had) walks through it carefully. And if old pain keeps coming back hard, talking it through with a pastor or a Christian counselor is wise, not weak.

## How do I start seeing God as a loving Father?

There are two ways to live with God. You can live like an orphan or like a son or daughter.

An orphan thinks: "Nobody is looking out for me. I have to earn my place. If I fail, I have to hide until I fix it." Every thought gets coated in fear and lack.

A child thinks: "I have a good Father. My place in this house is not up for debate. If I fail, I run to Him, not away from Him."

> **[Romans 8:15 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+8:15&version=NKJV)** "For you did not receive the spirit of bondage again to fear, but you received the Spirit of adoption by whom we cry out, 'Abba, Father.'"

Abba is a close family word for father. The Holy Spirit in you already calls God Abba. You are learning to agree with Him. The lesson [Crying Abba](/tracks/know-who-you-are/crying-abba) explains what it means.

Here are a few simple ways to start:

- **Read the Gospels with one question.** Each time you read about Jesus, ask, "What does this show me about the Father?" Start with Luke 15.
- **Talk to Him as Father.** Begin your prayers with "Father" or "Abba," and say it slowly. It may feel strange at first. Keep going.
- **Notice your picture when you fail.** The moment after you mess up is when your real picture of God shows up. Catch it, and compare it with Jesus.
- **Receive before you work.** Before you open your to-do list, take one minute to thank Him that you are already loved. Then work from that.

## Your "picture of the Father" worksheet

Take ten quiet minutes with this. Be honest in the first column. Then let Jesus correct the picture.

| When this happens | I usually picture God as... | What Jesus shows me | My new sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| I fail again | | He runs toward me (Luke 15:20) | |
| I need something | | He already knows (Matthew 6:8) | |
| I am corrected | | It is for my good (Hebrews 12:10) | |
| I feel unseen | | He rejoices over me (Zephaniah 3:17) | |
| I want to earn His love | | He loved me first (Matthew 3:17, 1 John 4:19) | |

An example of a new sentence: "When I fail, my Father is not crossing His arms. He is already running toward me."

Read your new sentences out loud once a day for a week. You are not trying to talk yourself into a feeling. You are renewing your mind with what is true.
