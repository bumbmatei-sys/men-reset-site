**Grace is what God already did for you through Jesus, given freely and not earned. It is His favor that forgives you and His power that changes you. Grace is not permission to keep sinning. It is the strength to stop.**

A lot of people hear the word grace and think it means "God lets it slide." Others hear it and get nervous, like it is a loophole that makes people careless. Both miss it. Grace is much bigger than a pass, and much stronger than a loophole.

## What does grace mean in the Bible?

Here is the simplest way I know to say it. Grace is what God did. Faith is your yes to what He did.

> **Ephesians 2:8-9 (NKJV)** "For by grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the gift of God, not of works, lest anyone should boast."

Notice where the work is. It is all on His side. Jesus lived the life you could not live, died the death you deserved, and rose again. You did not add anything to that. Your part is to believe it and receive it.

That is also why the Bible says without faith it is impossible to please God (Hebrews 11:6). Not because He is hard to please, but because without your yes, a gift just sits there unopened.

## Can you earn grace?

No. And the moment you try, you step out of it.

> **Romans 4:4-5 (NKJV)** "Now to him who works, the wages are not counted as grace but as debt. But to him who does not work but believes on Him who justifies the ungodly, his faith is accounted for righteousness."

Think of it like this. Imagine you are broke, and one day a lawyer calls. Someone very rich has died and left you everything. It is yours. You did nothing for it.

Now you have a few options. You can believe it, and ask the lawyer to walk you through how to actually access it. You can ignore the call and stay broke. Or you can decide you still need to do something to deserve it, which makes no sense. It would be like asking the man to die a second time so he could give you the same inheritance again.

That is what we do when we try to earn what Jesus already paid for. The Holy Spirit is the one who walks you through your inheritance (Romans 8:16-17). He shows you what is already yours.

## Does grace give you permission to sin?

No. Paul saw this question coming two thousand years ago.

> **Romans 6:1-2 (NKJV)** "What shall we say then? Shall we continue in sin that grace may abound? Certainly not! How shall we who died to sin live any longer in it?"

People often say it like this: *"Grace is not a license to sin. It is the power to stop."* And that is exactly how the Bible describes it.

> **Titus 2:11-12 (NKJV)** "For the grace of God that brings salvation has appeared to all men, teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live soberly, righteously, and godly in the present age."

Grace is a teacher. It does not just clean up the mess after you fall. It trains you to say no before you fall. If what someone calls grace makes you more comfortable with sin, it is not the grace of the Bible.

## If not the law, what makes you holy?

The law is good. It came from God. But it was never meant to make you holy.

The law works like a mirror. It shows you the dirt on your face. That is useful, because you need to know it is there. But nobody takes the mirror off the wall and tries to scrub their face with it. That is not what a mirror is for. You need water.

Rules can show you where you fall short. Only Jesus can wash you and give you a new heart. That is why Paul says sin shall not have dominion over you, because you are not under law but under grace (Romans 6:14). The power to change does not come from trying harder to keep the rules. It comes from being made new on the inside.

| Living by effort | Living by grace |
|---|---|
| "I have to be good enough for God to accept me." | "God accepted me in Christ, so I want to live for Him." |
| Hides after failing | Runs to God after failing |
| Counts good days and bad days | Looks at what Jesus finished |
| Says no to sin out of fear | Says no to sin out of love |
| Gets tired and gives up | Gets strength for the next step |

## Why do I still struggle if I am under grace?

Usually it is not because you need more grace. It is because you do not yet know what you already have.

> **Philemon 1:6 (NKJV)** "That the sharing of your faith may become effective by the acknowledgment of every good thing which is in you in Christ Jesus."

Read that slowly. Your faith becomes effective as you recognize what is already in you. Grace is unlimited on God's side. What limits it in your daily life is how much you know and believe about what He has done.

Peter says the same thing: "Grace and peace be multiplied to you in the knowledge of God and of Jesus our Lord" (2 Peter 1:2). Multiplied through knowing Him. Not through striving, not through being more special than anyone else. Peter even says we have received the same precious faith he had (2 Peter 1:1).

So if you keep falling in the same place, do not just ask God for more willpower. Ask Him to show you who you are in Christ. More on that in [your identity in Christ](/teachings/identity-in-christ-2026).

## How do you experience more of His grace?

Grace itself does not grow. It is already unlimited, and God is not holding any of it back. What grows is how much of it you experience as you grow. The Bible gives two simple keys. Simple, not instant.

1. **Faith.** Through Jesus "we have access by faith into this grace in which we stand" (Romans 5:2). Faith is the door. And faith grows by hearing and knowing His Word (Romans 10:17).
2. **Humility.** "God resists the proud, but gives grace to the humble" (James 4:6). This is where people get confused.

Pride is not only acting like you are better than everyone. It has another side too: *"I am nothing, I am worthless, God could never use me."* That sounds humble, but it is still all about you. Both are self-focused.

Real humility is believing what God says about you. If He says you are forgiven, you agree. If He says you are His child, you agree. If He says you are a new creation, you agree. Humility takes your eyes off yourself and puts them on Him.

## What does living by grace look like day to day?

It looks pretty normal, just with a different engine underneath.

- **Start the day receiving.** Before you ask God for anything, thank Him for what He already did. Read until something lights up, and stay there.
- **When you fall, run toward Him.** "Let us therefore come boldly to the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy and find grace to help in time of need" (Hebrews 4:16). Boldly. Not crawling, not after a week of feeling bad.
- **Say no from love, not fear.** When temptation shows up, remember who you belong to. You are not trying to stay in His family. You already are in it.
- **Ask for help in the moment.** "My grace is sufficient for you, for My strength is made perfect in weakness" (2 Corinthians 12:9). Weak moments are exactly where His strength shows up.
- **Make one practical change.** Grace does not mean you stop being wise. If something keeps tripping you, move it out of the way.
