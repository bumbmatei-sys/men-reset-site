**You are not what you keep doing. You are what Jesus says you already are.** God is not telling you what you need to become. He is telling you who you already are in Christ. When you keep falling, the answer is not to try harder. The answer is to see yourself the way the Father sees you, and let that change the way you live.

## Is what I'm doing my identity?

No. What you do is your behavior. Your identity is what God says about you after Jesus finished His work.

Many people live the other way around. They fall into the same sin again and then say "that is who I am". They say "I am an addict", "I am angry", "I am weak", "I am broken". In other words, they let the struggle become their name.

Don't let what you are going through become your identity.

If my face has pimples and I look in the mirror, the mirror shows me the truth. But I cannot take the mirror and rub it on my face to clean the pimples. That is not what the mirror is for. The law of God works the same way. It is holy and it is good. It shows you what is wrong. It has no power to make you right. Only the blood of Jesus can do that.

So the question is not "what have I done". The question is "what has He done, and who am I because of it".

## What happened to my old nature?

It died. That is not a metaphor. That is a legal fact in heaven.

> **Romans 6:6 (NASB)**, "knowing this, that our old self was crucified with Him, in order that our body of sin might be done away with."

> **2 Corinthians 5:17 (NASB)**, "Therefore if anyone is in Christ, this person is a new creation; the old things passed away; behold, new things have come."

Let's take a look at Romans 7:1-6. Paul compares it to marriage. A wife is bound to her husband as long as he lives. If the husband dies, she is free to be joined to another. Then Paul says this is exactly what happened to us. Our old nature died with Jesus. Now we are joined to Him. We are bringing forth fruit now to God.

So if you believe that after being born again you still carry a sin nature inside, you are saying the old husband is still alive. Which means you are living as if you are married to two at once. Paul calls that adultery.

You are not a sinner trying to be saved. You are a saint who sometimes sins. That is a big difference.

## Who am I in Christ?

This is what God says about you. He is not telling you what you need to receive. He is telling you what you already have.

**Who you are in Christ:**

- You are a new creation. The old things passed away. (2 Corinthians 5:17)
- You are dead to sin and alive to God. (Romans 6:11)
- You are a child of God, born of Him. (John 1:12)
- You are an heir together with Jesus. (Romans 8:17)
- You are the righteousness of God in Him. (2 Corinthians 5:21)
- You are accepted and beloved. (Ephesians 1:6)
- You are healed by His wounds. (1 Peter 2:24)
- You are forgiven, and your shame is taken away. (Hebrews 12:2)
- You are one spirit with Him. (1 Corinthians 6:17)
- You are complete in Him. (Colossians 2:10)

Read those slowly. Not as a wish list. As a fact list. Everything there is already yours in Jesus.

From identity comes calling, because who you are dictates what you do. Jesus healed because He is the Healer. He saved because He is the Savior. He forgave because He is the Forgiver. He did not do those things to become the Son of God. He did them because He is.

## Why did the devil attack Jesus' identity first?

Because he knew that if identity moves, everything else moves.

> **Matthew 4:3 (NASB)**, "And the tempter came and said to Him, 'If You are the Son of God, command that these stones become bread.'"

Notice the wording. He did not say "do something wrong". He said "if You are the Son of God". The first temptation was an attack on identity.

On the cross the same words came again. "If You are the Son of God, come down." (Matthew 27:40).

So when you hear in your mind "if you were really a Christian you would not do this", that is an old strategy. The devil has as much power as you give him by fixing your eyes on him. Look at Jesus instead. Your identity is settled in Him, not in your performance.

## How do I start living like the new person I already am?

By renewing your mind.

> **Romans 12:2 (NASB)**, "And do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind."

You are already new in your spirit. Your mind is the part that needs to catch up. When you believe a lie about yourself long enough, you start living like it is true. The renewing of the mind is going back to the truth until the truth is louder.

How do you do that practically?

- Meditate on the Word. Read what God says about you and stay there.
- Speak it out. Your mouth agrees with your spirit.
- Worship and pray in fellowship with the Holy Spirit. Make Him the priority, not the plan B.
- Take every thought that contradicts the Word and put it down.
- Surround yourself with people who call you up into who you are.

The truth makes us uncomfortable because we are comfortable with the lie. So give it time. If I wait too long before eating my food, it gets cold. Eat the truth now, not later.

## What do I do when I fall again?

You get up and you go back to Him. Right away. Not after you have punished yourself for three days.

Guilt says "I did something wrong". Shame says "something is wrong with me". Shame is a lie. Jesus did not only die for your sin. He hung naked on a cross for your shame. He went lower than you have ever gone. (Hebrews 12:2).

So when you fall:

- Confess it and be honest with Him. It is about positioning your heart back on Him.
- Receive His forgiveness as a fact, not as a feeling you have to earn.
- Do not live in fear of punishment. God is love, and love drives out fear. (1 John 4:18).
- Get back into fellowship with Him that same day.
- Look again at who you are, not at what you did.

The answer is both. You keep the standard of the Word and you rest in grace. Not to earn His love, but because you already have it.

## How did Jesus set me free?

I grew up in a Christian family but I had nothing to do with Jesus. By the age of 15 I was hooked on alcohol and smoking. I was going to school drunk. I was angry all the time. Under all that I was lonely and there was a huge emptiness inside of me. It turned into a deep depression. For a full year I wanted to die because I did not see a reason to live.

One night I was in my room, laying on my bed. I said a short and simple prayer: "Jesus if you really exist, help me." It came out of a crushed and broken heart. In that very moment I was instantly set free from depression. That emptiness inside of me was filled with a warm liquid love that I had never experienced before. The anger and the loneliness were gone for good. In a matter of moments I was a totally different person.

I also lived in shame because I was a porn addict. I felt like a failure. I had fears, like fear of failing and fear of disappointing the people around me. But Jesus took all of these things away. I felt like nothing, and Jesus saw value in me. I was a nobody but God made me His beloved son. There is so much peace in my mind now, peace I never had before.

Basically, He did it. I only asked for help.

## Where do I go from here?

Start with the truth about yourself, not with another round of trying harder. You are a new creation in Christ. Live from that, not toward it.
