**You build a relationship with the Holy Spirit by treating Him as the Person He is. He is not a force or a feeling. He is God, living in every believer, and Jesus called Him the Helper. Welcome Him in the morning, talk with Him through the day, ask Him to help you pray and to teach you as you read the Bible, and obey what He shows you. Over time, fellowship with Him becomes as natural as talking with a close friend.**

Many Christians are comfortable talking about the Father and about Jesus. But the Holy Spirit feels vague. Some picture Him as a kind of spiritual electricity. Others only think of Him when a church service gets loud. Many have never once spoken to Him directly.

I want to show you a different way. Not a new technique, but what the Bible says about who He is, and how simple it is to begin knowing Him.

## Who is the Holy Spirit?

The Holy Spirit is not an energy or an influence. He is a Person, the third Person of the Trinity, fully God with the Father and the Son.

You can see His personhood all through Scripture:

- **He has a mind.** "The Spirit searches all things, yes, the deep things of God" ([1 Corinthians 2:10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Corinthians+2:10&version=NKJV)).
- **He has a will.** He gives gifts "to each one individually as He wills" ([1 Corinthians 12:11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Corinthians+12:11&version=NKJV)).
- **He has feelings.** "And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God" ([Ephesians 4:30](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+4:30&version=NKJV)). You cannot grieve electricity.
- **He is God.** When Ananias lied to the Holy Spirit, Peter told him, "You have not lied to men but to God" ([Acts 5:3-4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts+5:3-4&version=NKJV)).

This matters, because you cannot have a relationship with a force. You can only use a force. But a Person can be known, loved and listened to.

One simple way I have come to see the Trinity: the Father is the source who plans and sends, the Son is the One who carried out the Father's will and showed us His heart, and the Holy Spirit is God's presence with us today, making the work of Jesus real in our lives. One God, three Persons, perfectly united.

## What is the role of the Holy Spirit in a believer's life?

Jesus told His disciples something surprising on the night before the cross:

> **[John 16:7 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+16:7&version=NKJV)** "Nevertheless I tell you the truth. It is to your advantage that I go away; for if I do not go away, the Helper will not come to you; but if I depart, I will send Him to you."

Better for them than having Jesus walking beside them? That is how much Jesus valued the Spirit's coming. Here is what the Helper does:

**He stays with you and lives in you.**

> **[John 14:16-17 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+14:16-17&version=NKJV)** "And I will pray the Father, and He will give you another Helper, that He may abide with you forever, the Spirit of truth, whom the world cannot receive, because it neither sees Him nor knows Him; but you know Him, for He dwells with you and will be in you."

"Another Helper" means another of the same kind as Jesus. And notice: "you know Him." Jesus expected His followers to know the Spirit personally.

**He teaches you and reminds you of what Jesus said** ([John 14:26](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+14:26&version=NKJV)).

**He guides you into truth and always points to Jesus.**

> **[John 16:13-14 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+16:13-14&version=NKJV)** "However, when He, the Spirit of truth, has come, He will guide you into all truth; for He will not speak on His own authority, but whatever He hears He will speak; and He will tell you things to come. He will glorify Me, for He will take of what is Mine and declare it to you."

The Holy Spirit is never the center of His own message. Real time with Him always leaves you loving Jesus more.

**He pours God's love into your heart.** "The love of God has been poured out in our hearts by the Holy Spirit who was given to us" ([Romans 5:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+5:5&version=NKJV)). God does not change you mainly by rules. He changes you by His love, poured in by the Spirit.

**He tells you whose child you are.** "The Spirit Himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God" ([Romans 8:16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+8:16-17&version=NKJV)). He is the One in you crying "Abba, Father" ([Galatians 4:6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+4:6&version=NKJV)).

**He helps you pray** when you do not know what to say ([Romans 8:26](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+8:26&version=NKJV)).

**He grows His fruit in you:** "love, joy, peace, longsuffering, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control" ([Galatians 5:22-23](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5:22-23&version=NKJV)).

**He gives you power to be a witness** ([Acts 1:8](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts+1:8&version=NKJV)).

### He convicts, but He does not accuse

Jesus said the Spirit "will convict the world of sin, and of righteousness, and of judgment" ([John 16:8](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+16:8&version=NKJV)). Then He explained each one (verses 9 to 11). Sin, because people do not believe in Jesus. Righteousness, because Jesus goes to the Father. Judgment, because the ruler of this world is already judged.

I find this freeing. The main sin the Spirit points to is not believing in Jesus. The righteousness He points to is the gift you receive through faith in Jesus, not something you produce. And the judgment He announces is the devil's, not yours. He is not following you around keeping a list. He is pointing you to Jesus again and again.

So when you do sin, His conviction feels like a hand on your shoulder turning you back home. Condemnation feels like a door closing. If the voice in your head only pushes you into hiding, that is not the Holy Spirit. The lesson [Accusation or Conviction](/tracks/renew-your-mind/accusation-or-conviction) helps you tell the difference.

## Can you have a relationship with the Holy Spirit?

Yes. Paul ends one of his letters with a blessing that names all three Persons of the Trinity:

> **[2 Corinthians 13:14 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Corinthians+13:14&version=NKJV)** "The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the communion of the Holy Spirit be with you all. Amen."

The word translated "communion" is the Greek word koinonia. It means fellowship, sharing, partnership. Paul is wishing for the believers what he himself lived: real fellowship with the Holy Spirit.

There is a difference between knowing about someone and knowing someone. You can believe true things about the Holy Spirit for years and never spend a single quiet minute with Him.

## What I took from Good Morning, Holy Spirit

One book that helped me see the Holy Spirit as a Person is *Good Morning, Holy Spirit* by Benny Hinn (Thomas Nelson, 1990). I recommend it to anyone who wants to know the Holy Spirit better. Here are the ideas from it that stayed with me, in my own words:

- **He is a Person you can know.** The book tells how Hinn moved from thinking of the Holy Spirit as a distant doctrine to meeting Him as Someone real, with a mind, a will and feelings.
- **Fellowship is more than requests.** Prayer often means asking for things. Fellowship is simply being together, the way two friends share breakfast without an agenda. The book invites you to spend time with the Holy Spirit just to be with Him.
- **He helps you pray to the Father.** Hinn describes learning to ask the Holy Spirit to help him before he prayed, and how that made talking with the Father in Jesus' name much easier. That matches [Romans 8:26](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+8:26&version=NKJV).
- **He is the Teacher of the Word.** The Bible opens up when you read it with the One who inspired it ([1 Corinthians 2:12](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Corinthians+2:12&version=NKJV)).
- **Start the day with Him.** The title comes from Hinn's habit of greeting the Holy Spirit first thing in the morning. It sounds simple, almost too simple. That is the point.

Read it the way you read any Christian book: with your Bible open, keeping what lines up with Scripture.

## How to build a relationship with the Holy Spirit

There is no formula here, and the Holy Spirit does not fit into a box. But there are simple habits that make room for Him.

### 1. Greet Him in the morning

> **[Psalm 5:3 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+5:3&version=NKJV)** "My voice You shall hear in the morning, O Lord; In the morning I will direct it to You, And I will look up."

Jesus Himself rose "a long while before daylight" to pray ([Mark 1:35](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+1:35&version=NKJV)). Before you check your phone, before the news, before the to-do list, say something like: "Good morning, Holy Spirit. Thank You that You are here. I want to walk with You today." It takes ten seconds. It changes who you think about first.

### 2. Talk with Him, not only about Him

Through the day, speak to Him simply. "Holy Spirit, help me be patient in this meeting." "What do You think about this?" "Thank You." You do not need special words. He already lives in you.

### 3. Ask Him to help you pray

When you sit down to pray and your mind is blank or scattered, start with: "Holy Spirit, help me pray." Then talk to the Father in Jesus' name. This is the pattern of the New Testament: to the Father, through the Son, by the Spirit ([Ephesians 2:18](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+2:18&version=NKJV)).

### 4. Read the Bible with Him

Before you read, ask Him to teach you. Then read slowly. When a verse stands out, stop there. That is often where He is speaking. The Word and the Spirit always agree. The Spirit without the Word is easy to confuse with your own feelings, and the Word without the Spirit can become just information. Together, they change you.

### 5. Listen, test, and obey quickly

When you sense a nudge to call someone, to apologize, to give, or to stop, test it. Does it line up with the Bible? Does it look like Jesus? Not everything that comes into your head is from the Spirit. Some of it is just you. That is why knowing your Bible and the character of Jesus matters so much. If it passes the test, obey it soon. Relationship grows in obedience.

### 6. Do not grieve Him

Right after "do not grieve the Holy Spirit," Paul lists what grieves Him: bitterness, wrath, anger, clamor, evil speaking and malice ([Ephesians 4:30-31](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+4:30-31&version=NKJV)). The more you love someone, the more you care about what hurts them. And when you do grieve Him, do not hide. Come back, tell Him, and receive forgiveness. He has not left.

### 7. Keep being filled

Paul says, "be filled with the Spirit" ([Ephesians 5:18](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+5:18&version=NKJV)). The sense is ongoing: keep on being filled. It is not a one-time stamp. In a dry season, do not panic. Get quiet, pray, open the Word, and ask Him to fill you again and to put the hunger back if it has faded.

## What about the gifts of the Holy Spirit?

The gifts of the Spirit are real, and they are given to serve other people. But I have seen many believers chase gifts and experiences while skipping the relationship. You do not need a special activation. What you need is closeness with Him: a humble place in your heart where you say, "I do not know much. Teach me to be like Jesus."

The fruit of the Spirit is the character of Jesus growing in you. If you seek Him, the fruit grows, and He will use you as He wills.

## Your 10-minute morning with the Holy Spirit

Here is a simple template. Print it, or copy it into your notes. Do it for seven mornings and see what changes.

| Minutes | What to do | What you can say | Done |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Greet Him before your phone | "Good morning, Holy Spirit. Thank You for being here." | ☐ |
| 2 | Thank the Father for one thing | "Father, thank You for..." | ☐ |
| 3 | Ask for help to pray | "Holy Spirit, help me pray today." | ☐ |
| 4 to 7 | Read a short passage slowly (start with John 14 to 16) | "Teach me. Show me Jesus here." | ☐ |
| 8 | Be quiet and listen | Write down one sentence that stood out. | ☐ |
| 9 | Name one place you need Him today | "Help me with... at..." | ☐ |
| 10 | Invite Him into the day | "I want to walk with You today. Let's go." | ☐ |

At the end of the day, look back for one minute: Where did I notice Him today? Where did I ignore Him? No guilt. Just learning to notice.
