**Most of the scary side effects of masturbation you see online are myths. It does not make you go bald, shrink your penis, weaken your bones or ruin your fertility. The real concerns are about patterns: losing control, shame and secrecy, porn, and the time it takes from your life.**

A viral thread lists 52 "horrible side effects of masturbation". Memory loss, hair loss, weak bones, schizophrenia, "death of brain", "male boobs", infertility and more. It ends with "stop or you have to die" and says not to listen to scientists. Lists like that spread fast, because they match what you already fear right after you fall.

So I checked the claims one by one against medical sources and published studies. I want you free from this habit. But you do not need false warnings to tell the truth. Fear is a weak teacher, and grace gives you a much stronger reason to change.

## Which claims are true and which are myths?

I grouped the 52 claims into the main themes. Each row links to its source so you can check it yourself.

| The claim | What the evidence says | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Losing control, a ruined career, missed responsibilities | True when it becomes compulsive. The WHO's ICD-11 lists compulsive sexual behaviour disorder (6C72): a persistent failure to control intense, repeated sexual urges that continues despite harm and causes real distress or impairment. [World Psychiatry, 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5775124/) | Supported |
| Guilt, shame, low self-esteem | Real and common. Research ties the distress mainly to feeling that your behavior goes against your values ("moral incongruence"), not to a physical effect. [Grubbs and Perry, 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29412013/) | Partly true |
| Depression and suicidal thoughts | Linked, but the direction is not clear. Believing you are "addicted" predicted distress over time more than use itself, and people who are already struggling may use it to cope. [Grubbs et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26372200/) and [Engelhardt et al., 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41559436/) | Partly true |
| Memory loss, brain fog, "death of brain" | No study shows masturbation damages the brain. One brain scan study linked more hours of porn with differences in reward areas, but the authors said this could be a result or something that was already there. [Kühn and Gallinat, 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24871202/) | Myth |
| Schizophrenia and other mental disorders | Cleveland Clinic lists mental illness among the myths about masturbation. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/24332-masturbation) | Myth |
| Erection problems, impotence, low libido | Cleveland Clinic says masturbation does not cause erectile dysfunction or low libido. With porn it is debated: some clinicians report problems that improved after stopping porn, while large surveys found little link. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/24332-masturbation), [Park et al., 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5039517/) and [Landripet and Štulhofer, 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25816904/) | Partly true |
| Premature ejaculation | Not shown as a direct cause. Mayo Clinic lists psychological factors such as anxiety, guilt that makes you rush and early sexual experiences, plus biological ones. [Mayo Clinic](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/premature-ejaculation/symptoms-causes/syc-20354900) | Myth |
| Thin sperm and infertility | Cleveland Clinic lists lower sperm count and infertility as myths. In a study of 9,489 semen samples, quality was best after short abstinence and dropped after long abstinence. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/24332-masturbation) and [Levitas et al., 2005](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15950636/) | Myth |
| Low testosterone, muscle loss, weak bones, "supplements stop working" | In a lab study, testosterone did not change with orgasm (it was somewhat higher after three weeks of abstinence). The popular "day 7 testosterone peak" study was retracted. I found no evidence for the bones or supplements claims. [Exton et al., 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11760788/) and [Jiang et al., 2003, retracted](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12659241/) | Myth |
| Constant fatigue, weakness, loss of vigor | Short-lived only. Orgasm raises prolactin, a hormone tied to feeling satisfied and winding down, and that passes. Lasting weakness is not shown. [Brody and Krüger, 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16095799/) | Partly true |
| Poor sleep | In a survey, people said they fell asleep more easily and slept better after orgasm, including through masturbation. This was self-reported. [Lastella et al., 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6409294/) | Myth |
| Semen leakage, "losing your vital fluid" | The fear that losing semen makes you weak has a name in psychiatry, "dhat" or semen-loss anxiety, and it has shown up in many cultures, including Britain and the US. [Sumathipala et al., 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14990517/) | Myth |
| Enlarged prostate | Mayo Clinic ties it to aging, family history and hormone changes. A large study even found men who ejaculated more often had a slightly lower prostate cancer risk, though that was observational. [Mayo Clinic](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/benign-prostatic-hyperplasia/symptoms-causes/syc-20370087) and [Rider et al., 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5040619/) | Myth |
| Weak vision, short height, small penis, hair loss, acne, dark skin, an "ugly face", "male boobs" | Cleveland Clinic lists vision loss and penis shrinkage as myths. KidsHealth says it will not stunt growth or harm eyesight. Mayo Clinic gives heredity, hormones and medical causes for hair loss, and hormone imbalance, medicines and other conditions for breast growth in men. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/24332-masturbation), [KidsHealth](https://kidshealth.org/en/teens/normal.html), [Mayo Clinic on hair loss](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/hair-loss/symptoms-causes/syc-20372926) and [Mayo Clinic on gynecomastia](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/gynecomastia/symptoms-causes/syc-20351793) | Myth |
| Migraines and headaches | Rare, but real. There is a recognized headache brought on by sexual activity. A sudden, severe headache needs a doctor the same day. [ICHD-3, 4.3](https://ichd-3.org/other-primary-headache-disorders/4-3-primary-headache-associated-with-sexual-activity/) | Partly true |

A few items on the list, like muscle cramps, back and shoulder pain, testicle and stomach pain, eye floaters, "smaller eyes", dark circles and "magnetism", had no solid study either way. So I left them out rather than guess. Guessing is how lists like that get made.

## What is a real concern, then?

Basically, the danger is not that your body falls apart. The danger is what the habit does to your freedom, your mind and your relationships.

### Losing control

Watch for the pattern. You keep doing it after you decided not to. It takes more time than you planned. You use it to escape stress, boredom or loneliness. And you hide it from the people closest to you.

When that pattern is persistent, continues despite harm, and causes real distress or impairment, the WHO calls it compulsive sexual behaviour disorder. That is a description of a pattern. It is not a verdict on who you are, and patterns can change.

### Shame, anxiety and depression

This link is real, but it does not run in one simple direction. Research found that believing you are "addicted" predicted distress more than the behavior itself. And people who already feel anxious, low or lonely often reach for the habit to numb it. Then shame and isolation push them back to the very thing they hate. It becomes a loop.

Please do not carry that alone. Talk to a pastor, a counselor or a doctor. If you have thoughts of suicide and you are in the US, call or text 988, or go to [988lifeline.org](https://988lifeline.org/). Outside the US, contact your local emergency number.

### Porn-related sexual problems

Here the evidence is honestly debated. Some doctors report erection problems and low desire with a real partner in men who use a lot of porn, and they say it improved after the men stopped. Large surveys have found little link. I will not pretend the science is settled.

What I can tell you is that porn is usually the bigger issue. It trains your mind on images that real life does not match. If you are going to cut one thing first, cut porn.

### Time, focus and relationships

Add up the hours in a month. Then add the mental energy it takes to keep a secret. Secrecy builds distance, with your spouse now or your future spouse later, and with friends and family. That cost is real, even if no study can measure your exact hours.

### Short-lived effects

You may feel tired or want to wind down afterward, or notice a little soreness. That is normal and it passes. It is not "loss of vigor", and it is not damage.

## Why do fear-based claims backfire?

Because fear feeds the shame cycle. You fall. You believe you have now damaged your brain or your body for good. You feel hopeless. You hide. And then you go back to the habit to numb that hopeless feeling. The warning becomes part of the trap.

There is a second problem. When someone finds out the claims are false, they often throw out the true warnings too. Once "it makes you go blind" turns out to be a myth, honest advice about compulsive use starts to sound fake as well.

Basically, truth is enough. You do not have to scare yourself into freedom. Guilt says "I did something wrong" and moves you toward Jesus. Shame says "Something is wrong with me" and makes you hide. I wrote more about that in [shame vs guilt](/teachings/shame-vs-guilt-jesus-2026).

## What is the real reason to stop?

Freedom, not fear. Galatians 5:1 says, "It was for freedom that Christ set us free." In Jesus you are a son or a daughter, not a slave (Galatians 4:7). And "if the Son makes you free, you will be free indeed" (John 8:36).

Purity is not about protecting your hairline. It is about honoring God with your body, which is a temple of the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 6:19-20), and guarding your heart from lust (Matthew 5:28). Your heavenly Father wants your heart and your mind whole, and He wants you present with the people you love.

And when you fall, there is "no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus" (Romans 8:1). God is not waiting to punish you. Grace is unlimited. As you grow, you experience more and more of it, but it was never small to begin with. You come to Him from forgiveness, not to earn it.
