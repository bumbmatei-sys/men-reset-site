**Being a child of God means you are born of Him, adopted into His family, and made His heir through faith in Jesus. You do not earn that place, and you do not keep it by performing. You receive it. Then you learn to live like a son or daughter instead of a servant: working from love instead of for it, running to the Father when you fail, and growing to look like Him.**

Many Christians can say "I am a child of God" without thinking twice. Fewer live like it. We sing it on Sunday and then spend the week like hired workers, trying to stay useful enough to keep our spot.

If that sounds like you, you are in good company. Jesus told a whole story about it.

## What does it mean to be a child of God?

The Bible describes three things that happen at once.

**You are born of God.** Jesus said that unless one is born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God ([John 3:3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+3:3&version=NKJV)). Being a child of God is not a nice way to describe a good person. It is a new birth.

> **[2 Corinthians 5:17 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Corinthians+5:17&version=NKJV)** "Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; old things have passed away; behold, all things have become new."

**You are adopted.** In the Roman world, an adopted son had the same standing as a natural son. Paul uses that picture on purpose:

> **[Ephesians 1:5 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+1:5&version=NKJV)** "having predestined us to adoption as sons by Jesus Christ to Himself, according to the good pleasure of His will,"

"Sons" here includes daughters. It is about standing in the family, not about gender. And notice the reason: "the good pleasure of His will." He wanted you.

**You are an heir.**

> **[Romans 8:16-17 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+8:16-17&version=NKJV)** "The Spirit Himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God, and if children, then heirs, heirs of God and joint heirs with Christ, if indeed we suffer with Him, that we may also be glorified together."

Joint heirs with Christ. Whatever belongs to Jesus as the Son, you share in Him. The lesson [Adopted for the Full Inheritance](/tracks/know-who-you-are/adopted-for-the-full-inheritance) unpacks what that includes.

## How do you become a child of God?

> **[John 1:12 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+1:12&version=NKJV)** "But as many as received Him, to them He gave the right to become children of God, to those who believe in His name:"

Receive and believe. That is the whole door. Not clean up first, not prove you are serious, not wait until you feel worthy.

> **[Galatians 3:26 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+3:26&version=NKJV)** "For you are all sons of God through faith in Christ Jesus."

If you have never done this, you can talk to Jesus right now in your own words. Tell Him you need Him, that you believe He died and rose again for you, and that you want to belong to Him. He does not reject anyone who comes.

## Is everyone a child of God?

Every person is made by God, carries His image, and is loved by Him. But Scripture uses "children of God" for those who have received Jesus. That is not meant to shut people out. It is meant to show you the way in. The door is open, and it is open to everyone.

## Servant or son: the two brothers in Luke 15

You probably know the story of the lost son. A young man takes his inheritance early, wastes it, and ends up feeding pigs. Then he decides to go home. Listen to the speech he prepares:

> **[Luke 15:19 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+15:19&version=NKJV)** "and I am no longer worthy to be called your son. Make me like one of your hired servants."

He was ready to come back, but only as a worker. That is how many of us return to God after we fail. "I know I can't be a son anymore, but maybe I can earn a spot in the back."

The father does not even let him finish. He runs, embraces him, and says:

> **[Luke 15:22 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+15:22&version=NKJV)** "Bring out the best robe and put it on him, and put a ring on his hand and sandals on his feet."

The robe is honor. The ring is authority in the family. Servants often went barefoot. Sandals marked a son of the house. In one moment, the father puts him back in his full place. No probation period.

Then comes the part many people skip. The older brother, who never left home, is angry and will not come in to the celebration. Listen to how he talks:

> **[Luke 15:29 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+15:29&version=NKJV)** "Lo, these many years I have been serving you; I never transgressed your commandment at any time; and yet you never gave me a young goat, that I might make merry with my friends."

Serving. Commandments. Never got paid. He lived in the house for years, but in his heart he was a servant too. The younger son thought he had lost his place. The older son thought he had to earn his place. Both missed the father.

And the father answers the older son with some of the most beautiful words in the Bible:

> **[Luke 15:31 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+15:31&version=NKJV)** "Son, you are always with me, and all that I have is yours."

That is the voice of the heavenly Father to you, whichever brother you are today.

> **[Galatians 4:7 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+4:7&version=NKJV)** "Therefore you are no longer a slave but a son, and if a son, then an heir of God through Christ."

## What changes when you know you are a son or daughter?

**You belong for good.** Jesus said, "a slave does not abide in the house forever, but a son abides forever" ([John 8:35](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+8:35&version=NKJV)). A servant can be let go. A son cannot be un-born.

**You come close without fear.** You have "access by one Spirit to the Father" ([Ephesians 2:18](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+2:18&version=NKJV)), and you can "come boldly to the throne of grace" ([Hebrews 4:16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Hebrews+4:16&version=NKJV)). Servants wait at the door. Children walk in.

**The Spirit in you calls Him Father.**

> **[Galatians 4:6 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+4:6&version=NKJV)** "And because you are sons, God has sent forth the Spirit of His Son into your hearts, crying out, 'Abba, Father!'"

You did not make this happen by trying. It is already going on inside you. More on that in the lesson [Crying Abba](/tracks/know-who-you-are/crying-abba).

**You work from rest.** A servant works to get approval. A son works because he already has it. The work might look the same from the outside. The heart behind it is completely different. That is the heart of the lesson [Working From Rest](/tracks/know-who-you-are/working-from-rest).

## Can a child of God still sin?

Yes. Being a child of God does not mean you never stumble.

> **[1 John 2:1 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+John+2:1&version=NKJV)** "My little children, these things I write to you, so that you may not sin. And if anyone sins, we have an Advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous."

Look at how John starts: "My little children." He writes to believers who might sin, and he still calls them children. Sin is serious. It hurts you and the people around you, and the Father will correct you. But correction is for children. It is proof you belong, not proof you are out.

What changes is your relationship with sin. Something I have noticed in my own life: the more I walk with God and see His love, the more grateful I become, and the less I want to sin at all. Not because of rules, but because I do not want anything between me and my Father.

## How should a child of God live?

Jesus said we must "become as little children" ([Matthew 18:3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+18:3&version=NKJV)). When I think about what a child of God looks like, a few things stand out:

- **Dependent on the Father.** Children do not pretend to be self-made.
- **In love with the Father.** They want to be near Him, not just get things from Him.
- **Seeking time with Him.** They keep coming back to where He is.
- **Humble.** They are not trying to look bigger than they are.
- **Imitating the Father.** "Be imitators of God as dear children" ([Ephesians 5:1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+5:1&version=NKJV)).
- **Trusting what He says.** His word settles things, even when feelings disagree.

And one day the picture will be complete:

> **[1 John 3:2 (NKJV)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+John+3:2&version=NKJV)** "Beloved, now we are children of God; and it has not yet been revealed what we shall be, but we know that when He is revealed, we shall be like Him, for we shall see Him as He is."

"Now we are." Not someday. Now.

Read each line and tick the ones that sound like you lately. Then read the truth next to it out loud. This is not a test you can fail. It is a mirror, so you can see where you are still thinking like a servant.

Pick the one line you ticked that bothers you most. Write the truth on a card or in your phone, and read it every morning this week.
