Many men hear "lead your family" and feel one of two things. Some feel a weight they think they can never carry. Others feel permission to be in charge. Both miss what Jesus meant.

This lesson is about the starting point. Before you read the Bible with your kids or plan a date with your wife, you need to know what kind of leader you are being asked to be.

## What does it mean to be the spiritual leader of the home?

It means you go first.

You go first in love. You go first in prayer. You go first in saying sorry. You go first in doing the dishes, getting up with the baby, turning off the screen, and asking how everyone is really doing.

It does not mean you make every decision alone. It does not mean your wife and children exist to serve your plans. It does not mean you are closer to God than they are.

Paul writes, "Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ also loved the church and gave Himself for her" (Ephesians 5:25). Look at what Christ did. He did not demand. He gave. The measure of leadership in your home is how much you give, not how much you get.

## What did Jesus say about leadership?

James and John once asked Jesus for the best seats in His kingdom. The other disciples were upset. So Jesus gathered them and said this:

"You know that those who are considered rulers over the Gentiles lord it over them, and their great ones exercise authority over them. Yet it shall not be so among you; but whoever desires to become great among you shall be your servant" (Mark 10:42-43).

Then He pointed to Himself. "For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give His life a ransom for many" (Mark 10:45).

The Gospel of Mark shows Jesus as the servant who gives His life for the world. Right in the middle of that story He tells His friends that this is how they are to lead too. That is the pattern for your home. On the night before the cross, He knelt and washed His disciples' feet, and then He said, "I have given you an example, that you should do as I have done to you" (John 13:15).

## Who should be the spiritual leader of the home?

The Bible gives the husband a real responsibility in his home. That responsibility is a call to serve, protect, and care, not a ranking. Paul writes in the same passage that believers are to be "submitting to one another in the fear of God" (Ephesians 5:21). Your wife is a fellow heir "of the grace of life" (1 Peter 3:7). She is not under you in God's love. She has her own walk with Him, her own wisdom, and her own voice.

If you are a single father, a stepfather, or a man preparing for marriage, this is still for you. The call is the same. Go first in love.

## What does leadership look like on an ordinary Tuesday?

It is quieter than most men expect.

- You notice your wife is tired, and you take the kids for an hour without being asked.
- You pray for your family by name, even for two minutes.
- You ask her opinion on a decision and actually change your mind when she is right.
- You keep your word about small things. You said you would fix the door, so you fix the door.
- You are the first one to say, "I was wrong. I'm sorry."

None of this is dramatic. That is the point. Love is mostly made of small, repeated choices.

## Control or love: what is the difference?

Some men grew up with a father who led by fear. Some have picked up ideas online that a real man controls his home. Let us be plain. Control is not biblical leadership.

Control uses fear, pressure, guilt, money, silence, or anger to get its way. Love uses truth, patience, service, and presence. Control needs to win. Love wants the other person to flourish.

"Love suffers long and is kind; love does not envy; love does not parade itself, is not puffed up; does not behave rudely, does not seek its own, is not provoked, thinks no evil" (1 Corinthians 13:4-5).

| Decisions | I decide and announce it | I ask, listen, and decide together | |

Pick the one row that stings the most. That is where your leading starts.

## Should a Christian husband discipline his wife?

No. Some people search for this, so it needs a clear answer. A husband has no right to punish, hit, frighten, or control his wife. Nothing in Christ's love for the church looks like that. Paul writes, "Husbands, love your wives and do not be bitter toward them" (Colossians 3:19).

If you have hurt, threatened, or frightened your wife or children, get help today, and get emergency help if anyone is in danger. Do not ask anyone to keep it quiet. If you feel very low or unsafe yourself, tell someone and get help right away. A Christian counselor is wisdom, not weak faith.

## What if I have never led my family well?

Then you are where many men begin. The good news is not that you have been good enough. It is that your heavenly Father is patient, and His mercies "are new every morning" (Lamentations 3:23).

You do not have to announce a new era to your family. Do not give a big speech. Just start serving. They will notice the change long before they believe a promise.
