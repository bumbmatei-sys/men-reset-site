Most people ask the question the wrong way round. They ask, "How do I get God to love me?" Or, "What do I need to fix before He does?"

So let's start with the answer. You do not get Him to love you. He already does. The real question is whether you will let it land.

## Look where He showed it

Feelings come and go. Some days you feel close to God and some days you feel nothing. If your proof of His love is a feeling, you will doubt it every bad week.

So God did not leave it to your feelings. He put it on display. Romans 5:8 says He demonstrated His love while we were still sinners. Not after we cleaned up. Not after a good month. While.

Think about what that means. At the cross, you had done nothing for Him. You had not prayed, served, or quit anything. And Jesus still went. So your best day cannot make Him love you more, and your worst day cannot make Him love you less. It was settled before you showed up.

## It comes from who He is

People love based on the person in front of them. They love you more when you perform and less when you let them down. Most of us learned love that way, so we assume God works the same.

He does not. 1 John 4:8 says God is love. It is not a mood He is in. It is who He is. The Father, Jesus, and the Holy Spirit have loved each other forever, before the world existed. You were not created to fill a hole in God. You were invited into a love that was already full.

That is why the Bible uses a rare word for it, agape. It is a love decided by the one giving it, not earned by the one receiving it.

## Made to love Him back

So why did God make people at all? Not because He was lonely, and not because He needed workers. He made us in His image so there could be love going both ways.

That is why He put a tree in the garden He told Adam not to eat from. It looks strange at first. But love that cannot say no is not love. It is programming. God took the risk that we would walk away, because He wanted a real yes.

1 John 4:19 puts it simply. We love because He first loved us. He always moves first. Your part is not to start the relationship. It is to answer it.

That takes pressure off. You are not trying to get God's attention. You already have it. You are responding to Someone who loved you before you could say a word back.

## Jesus' order

Most of the world runs on believe, behave, belong. Get your beliefs right, clean up your behavior, and then you can join us.

Watch what Jesus did. In John 1, two men started following Him and nervously asked where He was staying. He did not test them. He said, "Come and see." They spent the day with Him. Belonging came first. Belief grew from being near Him. Behavior changed over time.

That is still His order for you. You do not have to fix yourself to come close. You come close, and He changes you.

If shame is the voice telling you to stay away until you are better, read [Shame vs Guilt](/teachings/shame-vs-guilt-jesus-2026). Guilt points at something you did. Shame says you are the problem. Jesus deals with both.

## Receiving is the skill

So how do you actually know God loves you? You receive it. Like a gift, not a bill.

That sounds easy. For a lot of us it is the hardest thing. We are good at doing. We are bad at being given to. Receiving means you are not in control. It means admitting you need it.

Think of a garden hose. The water source is fine. If nothing comes out, something is blocking it somewhere. God's love is not the problem. Usually something on our end, like fear, shame, or busyness, is pinching the flow. We will look at those blockages [later in this course](/tracks/know-gods-love/why-you-cant-feel-it).

For now, just practice the posture. Open hands. Stop for a moment and let Him be the one doing something.

## What changes

When you live from His love, a few things start to shift.

You stop squeezing people for a love only God can give. You can enjoy their love without asking it to replace His.

You stop panicking every time things go wrong, because your worth is not on the line.

And you start obeying for a different reason. Not to keep Him, but because you already have Him.

## Do this today

Once a day this week, sit for two minutes and ask for nothing. Say, "Father, You loved me before I did anything today." Then stay quiet.

Pay attention to what pushes back. Maybe it is "Not after what I did yesterday." Maybe "I need to earn this." Write that thought down. You are not trying to fix it yet. You are finding what is blocking the flow.

## Reflect

Where are you still doing things to be loved, instead of doing them because you already are?
