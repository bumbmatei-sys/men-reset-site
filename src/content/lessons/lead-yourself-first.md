Most men want to lead their family from the front. They want to be the one who starts the prayer, sets the tone, and gives good advice. That is good. But there is a step before that, and it happens when nobody is watching.

## Where we are in this course

In lesson 1 you saw that leading your home means going first in love and service. This lesson is about going first with God. You cannot lead people somewhere you are not going yourself.

## How do I become the spiritual leader of my home?

Paul told a young pastor, "Take heed to yourself and to the doctrine. Continue in them, for in doing this you will save both yourself and those who hear you" (1 Timothy 4:16). Notice the order. Yourself first. Then those who hear you.

Your family will learn more from what you do in the morning than from anything you say at dinner. A child who sees his father with an open Bible, or hears him pray when things go wrong, learns that God is real in this house.

So the first step is not a family devotional. It is your own.

## Start with ten minutes

Jesus, the Son of God, made time alone with His Father. "Now in the morning, having risen a long while before daylight, He went out and departed to a solitary place; and there He prayed" (Mark 1:35).

You may have a job, a commute, small children who wake up early, or a body that is tired. That is fine. Start with ten minutes.

1. **Same time, same place.** Before the phone. A kitchen chair, a car in the driveway, a quiet corner.
2. **One short passage.** A psalm, a few verses from a Gospel. Read it slowly.
3. **Pray simply.** Thank Him. Tell Him the truth about yourself. Pray for each person in your home by name.
4. **One line to carry.** Pick one sentence from what you read and take it into your day.

If you want help building a morning that holds, the [Discipline and a Morning Routine](/tracks/discipline-and-morning-routine) course walks through it step by step. If you work long hours, read [a Christian morning routine for men with a job](/teachings/christian-morning-routine-men-2026).

## You lead as a son, not as a performer

Here is a trap. A man decides to lead his family, and it turns into another performance. He tries harder, feels guilty when he misses, and starts to feel like God is disappointed in him.

That is not how this works. You do not pray to earn your Father's approval. You pray because you already have it. Work done as a son who is already loved is very different from work done to try to be loved. One is rest. The other is a treadmill.

Jesus said, "Come to Me, all you who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest" (Matthew 11:28). Lead from that rest. If this is hard for you to believe, the [Know Who You Are](/tracks/know-who-you-are) course is a good place to settle it.

## What about my hidden habits?

What you do alone shapes your home. If you are up past midnight scrolling, the morning does not happen, and your patience at breakfast is thin. If porn or a secret habit has a grip on you, shame will keep you from leading with a clear heart.

That does not disqualify you. It means you bring it into the light. "He who covers his sins will not prosper, But whoever confesses and forsakes them will have mercy" (Proverbs 28:13). Tell your heavenly Father. Then tell one trusted man.

If you keep falling and do not know how to pray anymore, read [How to Pray When You Keep Falling](/teachings/how-to-pray-when-you-keep-falling). If you need someone to walk with you, start with [How to Find a Christian Accountability Partner](/teachings/christian-accountability-partner).

## Do I need to know the Bible well to lead my family?

No. You need to be reading it. You do not have to be a scholar. You have to be honest and willing to say, "I don't know, let's find out together." That kind of humility is part of leading.

## What if my wife is further along spiritually than I am?

That is a gift, not a threat. Thank God for her. Learn from her. Leading is not about being the most spiritual person in the room. It is about taking responsibility to keep your own walk alive and to care for the walk of those around you. Ask her to pray for you. That is a strong thing for a man to do.

## When should I get more help?

If you feel low most days, cannot sleep, or have thoughts of harming yourself, please do not try to push through alone. Call or text **988**. Talk to your doctor or a licensed Christian counselor. Getting help is wisdom, not weak faith.
