There will come a day when the crisis feels far away. She laughs again. You have a normal evening. Weeks go by without a hard conversation. It will feel like the work is done.

That is the most important day in this whole course. Because that is when many men quietly stop.

## Where we are in this course

In lesson 5, [Your Shame Is Not Her Job](/tracks/rebuilding-trust-after-porn/your-shame-is-not-her-job), we learned to carry our shame to God and a brother instead of handing it to our wives. Now we lift our eyes from the hard weeks to the long road.

## How do you rebuild broken trust in a Christian marriage?

Slowly, and with God at the center.

"Most men will proclaim each his own goodness, But who can find a faithful man?" (Proverbs 20:6). Many men can be good for a weekend. Many can make a moving speech. What is rare is a man who keeps being faithful, year after year, when no one is watching and no one is clapping.

God is looking for faithfulness. Not brilliance, not a dramatic turnaround story. Faithfulness in character and in integrity. It is not exciting. It is often overlooked. But it is the thing your wife is waiting to see, and it is the thing your heavenly Father loves to build in His sons.

A Christian marriage has one more thing going for it. You are not only trying to become trustworthy to her. You are walking with a God who is trustworthy to both of you. "Through the Lord's mercies we are not consumed, Because His compassions fail not. They are new every morning; Great is Your faithfulness" (Lamentations 3:22-23). Your faithfulness grows out of His.

## What a trustworthy life looks like over time

It looks ordinary. That is the point.

It looks like a phone she could pick up at any time and find nothing hidden. It looks like coming home when you said. Like telling your brother the truth for the hundredth Thursday in a row. Like apologizing quickly for small things. Like being the same man at home that you are at church.

Psalm 15 describes the man who may dwell with the Lord. One line stands out: "He who swears to his own hurt and does not change" (Psalm 15:4). He keeps his word even when it costs him. That is the kind of man who becomes trustworthy. Not the one who never struggles, but the one whose word holds.

## Keep the practices after the crisis fades

The things that helped in the hard months are the things to keep. Your filter. Your open phone. Your brother. Your morning time with God. Your counselor, for as long as they say it is wise.

Most drift does not begin with a dramatic choice. It begins when things feel normal and the practices start to feel unnecessary. You skip one check-in. Then two. You stop mentioning small temptations because it seems silly now. That is how old patterns come back.

Talk with her and your counselor about which practices to keep for good and which can change as trust grows. Let it be a shared decision, not you deciding on your own that you have earned more freedom.

## Closeness returns at her pace

As trust grows, closeness may return. Emotional closeness. Spiritual closeness, praying together again. Physical closeness too.

All of it at her pace. Do not pressure her. Do not treat her affection as a reward you have earned or as proof that the work is over. Peter wrote, "Husbands, likewise, dwell with them with understanding, giving honor to the wife" (1 Peter 3:7). Understanding means you do not push. Honor means you treat her heart as precious, not as a box to tick.

A counselor can help you both talk about this part honestly and safely.

## If she is still not sure

Some wives take a very long time. Some are not sure they can stay. That is her decision before God, and it is not yours to control or argue her out of. Do not use guilt, Scripture, or the kids as pressure.

Whatever she decides, your faithfulness is first to your heavenly Father. Keep walking with Him. Keep being the man He is making you, whether she is watching or not. "He who has begun a good work in you will complete it until the day of Jesus Christ" (Philippians 1:6). He has not given up on you, and He has not given up on her.

## What this course was really about

This course was never about getting your wife to trust you again by a certain date. It was about becoming a faithful man. Her trust may follow. Pray that it does. But your faithfulness is worth building either way, because it honors your heavenly Father and it is who He made you to be.

If you want to go deeper into who you are as His son, the course [Know Who You Are](/tracks/know-who-you-are) is a good next step. And if your thought life is still the main battle, [Renew Your Mind](/tracks/renew-your-mind) goes one step at a time.

## Do this today

Pick the practices from this course you will keep for the next twelve weeks and write them at the top of the tracker. Put a reminder in your calendar for the same time each week to look back honestly. Tell your brother the plan, so someone checks on it when the crisis feels far away.
