An urge is a ten-minute skill. The urge feels like it will last all night and like you cannot think until you give in. Both feelings lie. If you do not feed it, the peak drops.

Basically, your job is to not feed it while it is loud. That is all.

This is the lesson to open when you are already in it. So read it once now, when you are calm. Later the steps should be boring.

## Do not debate

A debate inside the urge is the urge talking to itself.

"Just one minute." "You earned it." "You will quit tomorrow." "Let me just check if it is still there." Every one of those is enticement. That is the old word in the verse above. You are being drawn away.

You do not need a better argument. You need a change of place.

If you are holding the phone, put it down on a table, not in your hand. If you are sitting where the habit usually happens, stand up. The script starts with the feet.

## Run the same three lines

1. I leave this spot.
2. I do one physical thing.
3. I tell the truth in one sentence, out loud or in a note.

Physical things that work for most people: walk outside or to another room, drink a full glass of water, ten pushups or one flight of stairs, cold water on the face, a shower with a plan to get dressed right after. Pick yours in advance. New ideas are not the point. Repetition is.

The sentence can be dull. "Urge. Left the office. Did not open it." Send it to your witness if you have one. If you do not, the note still counts, but you still need a person soon. See [Building a New Default](/tracks/break-free/porn-addiction-recovery-for-men). That lesson is for anyone, not only men.

## Refuse the test look

The test look is how the urge gets paid.

You tell yourself you are only checking whether you still want it. You do. The image or the first sip or the first level does the rest. There is no safe thumbnail, no safe "just the menu." Close it. Do not clear the thought by glancing.

If it comes to you without your search, a chat, an ad, a clip in a feed, you still do not stay. You leave the app. Staying to be disgusted is still staying.

## Let the clock work

Set a timer for ten minutes if you need proof. Do the script. Do not sit there and monitor the feeling.

When the timer ends, start a normal task with a finish line. Trash out. Email sent. Lunch made. Kids picked up. The urge can still mutter. You are just not in the chair it owns.

If it returns an hour later, run the script again. That is not failure. That is the skill.

## After a strong one

You may feel proud, or you may feel flat. Both are normal.

Do not reward a survived urge with a smaller version of the habit. And do not punish a survived urge with a speech about how weak you are for wanting it. Wanting it means the old training is still in you. Leaving means the training is getting interrupted.

## Three places, same script

In bed: the phone is already in your hand. Put it on the floor, out of reach. Better, it was never there. Then sit up. Feet on the floor. The lie is "I will just finish this thought lying down." Sit up first. Thoughts are easier to leave when your body has left.

In the bathroom: if the phone came in with you, that was already the mistake. Set it outside next time. This time, finish and leave. Do not think "since I am here." Since I am here is the whole trap.

At a desk: stand and walk to a window, a printer, a glass of water. Anything that makes you pass another person if you can. Urges hate witnesses. You do not have to announce it. Being in a hallway is enough to break the private spell.

If the urge shows up with a picture or a memory you did not choose, do not study it. Call it "urge." Run the three lines. Go back to the task in front of you. Staring until it fades is still staring.

Practice once today while you are calm. Walk the route from the danger spot to the other room. It feels awkward and that is fine. In the moment you will not be inventive. You will do what your feet already did this afternoon.

## If it lasts longer than ten minutes

Some waves come back. Run the script again. Do not invent a new one. A second walk is not a failure of the first walk. It means the cue is still in the room, so you leave again.

If you can change the rest of the evening, do that. Go to a store. Sit with other people. Start a chore that dirties your hands. Staying in the danger room to see if it passed is how it comes back.

Do not open a "harmless" version to take the edge off. That is feeding. The edge comes off by time and by a different occupation, not by a smaller dose of the same thing.

If you need a voice, call someone about anything ordinary. You do not have to say the word urge unless you want to. The point is to not be alone with the script.

If urges are constant and they come with despair, rage, or a home you are afraid to go back to, get help that is bigger than a webpage. Use the script tonight anyway. It works together with better help, not against it.
