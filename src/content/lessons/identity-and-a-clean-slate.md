You are not starting this month as a project that has to earn Jesus. Basically, He already washed you. The habit can be porn, the phone, a game, a drink, a substance, or another loop. The standing is the same. You are clean in Him. So the next seven days are foot washing. Dust off the feet. Do not climb back into the bath as if the first one failed.

## Day 1. The bath and the foot washing

John 13:10. Anyone who has bathed needs only to wash his feet and is clean all over.

**Declaration.** I have been fully washed by the blood of Jesus. My standing before God is clean, permanent and secure.

**Do this.**

- Take a sheet of paper.
- Write every guilt-thought and past failure that haunts you.
- Across the whole page write: **BATHED IN FULL. POSITIONALLY CLEAN.**
- Tear it up and throw it away.

**Prayer.** Lord Jesus, I am fully justified by Your blood. Wash my feet today from any dust of compromise or doubt. I walk into this day clean and loved. Amen.

## Day 2. No condemnation

Romans 8:1. There is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus.

God is not angry with you. He loves you to the last and highest degree. So shame does not get to run the room.

**Declaration.** I live in a no-condemnation zone. God is not angry with me. He loves me to the last and highest degree.

**Do this.** Find the room or place where you feel the most shame or temptation. Walk in. Raise your hand and say out loud: **This space is governed by the zero-condemnation law of Christ.**

**Prayer.** Father, I refuse shame and self-hate today. I take Your love and rest in it as Your child. Amen.

## Day 3. Clothed in righteousness

2 Corinthians 5:21. For He made Him who knew no sin to be sin for us, that we might become the righteousness of God in Him.

Right now you are as righteous before God as Jesus is, because you wear His righteousness. That is not a mood. That is what He did.

**Declaration.** Right now I am as righteous before God as Jesus is, because I wear His righteousness.

**Do this.** Stand in front of a mirror. Look yourself in the eye and say it three times: **You are the righteousness of God in Christ Jesus. Stop acting like a slave.**

**Prayer.** Lord, my acceptance rests on what Jesus did, not on my performance. I wear His righteousness today with thanks. Amen.

## Day 4. Seated above the habit

Ephesians 2:4-6. But God, who is rich in mercy, raised us up together, and made us sit together in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus.

You are seated with Jesus, far above every habit and every trigger. The urge is real. Your seat is higher.

**Declaration.** I am seated with Jesus in heavenly places, far above every habit and every trigger.

**Do this.** The moment an urge or impure thought hits today, sit down in a chair. Close your eyes. Picture where you really are: seated with Jesus, above the temptation.

**Prayer.** Father, line up my view with where I really am in Jesus. From that seat I rule over my lower appetites. Amen.

## Day 5. Adopted into the King's family

Galatians 4:6. God has sent forth the Spirit of His Son into your hearts, crying out, "Abba, Father!"

You are not a slave to your impulses or your screens. You are an adopted child of the King.

**Declaration.** I am not a slave to my impulses or my screens. I am an adopted child of the King.

**Do this.** Audit your digital life. Delete any hidden app, secret vault, or browser setting that belongs to a slave identity.

**Prayer.** Abba Father, thank You for adopting me into Your family. Tune my heart to hear Your voice today. Amen.

## Day 6. The cleansing blood

1 John 1:9. If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.

The blood of Jesus keeps cleansing your conscience. Darkness has no hiding place. Basically, you tell Him the truth the moment it shows up. You do not wait for a better speech.

**Declaration.** The blood of Jesus keeps cleansing my conscience. Darkness has no hiding place in my life.

**Do this.** Practice instant honesty. When you feel an impure pull, bring it to God right away. Confess it. Do not hide it and do not explain it away.

**Prayer.** Lord, I bring every private thought into Your light. Wash my feet with Your truth and keep my walk open before You. Amen.

## Day 7. Sabbath rest

Hebrews 4:9-10. There remains therefore a rest for the people of God. For he who has entered His rest has himself also ceased from his works.

Your victory does not come from striving. It comes from resting in the finished work of Jesus.

**Declaration.** My victory does not come from striving. It comes from resting in the finished work of Jesus.

**Do this.** Take a strict 24-hour Sabbath. Step away from performance pressure, work email, and mental striving. Rest your body and your soul.

**Prayer.** Lord, I stop my self-effort today. I receive Your rest deep into my soul. Amen.
