**How to reset your brain from porn** is a phrase people search when they want the old pull to quiet down. Maybe that is you. Maybe your habit is the phone, gaming, alcohol, or something else that keeps pulling you back. It is the same problem. Your attention got trained on a fast hit, and now the slow things feel boring.

Be careful with anyone who sells you a scan, a supplement, or a magic number of days. I will not invent a timeline here. I will tell you what you can actually repeat.

Basically, a reset is training your attention to stay with real, slower things long enough that the hit is no longer the default. That takes days you can describe, not a slogan.

## Give attention a job

The verse for this lesson is a list, not a feeling. True, honest, just, pure, lovely, worth repeating.

You do not have to stare at a sunset and call it holiness. Haha. You do have to stop handing every spare minute to a feed designed to escalate.

So pick what "think on these things" means in your house. A chapter of Scripture if that is your practice. A book that is not about hustle. A walk without earbuds once this week. Work done fully for twenty minutes. A conversation where you ask a question and wait. The content matters, and so does the fact that you stayed.

If you only delete the habit and keep a firehose of clips, the mind keeps the same motion. The [phone and gaming track](/tracks/quit-gaming-and-the-phone) will go after that firehose. For now, start with one block of twenty quiet minutes.

## Sleep is part of the reset

A short night makes novelty feel urgent. You will "just check" because you are dull and wired at the same time. I know that feeling.

Protect sleep like it is part of quitting. Because it is. Phone out of the bedroom. A consistent time. Caffeine early enough that it is not still running the show at midnight. If you drink to come down at night, notice that. Do not pretend it is unrelated. Alcohol is on the map of the [30-day reset](/tracks/30-day-reset) for that reason.

You may dream more, notice more attraction, or feel flat for a while. Those are common reports from people who stop. They are not a diagnosis from this page. If your mood, sleep, or despair gets worse and stays worse, talk to a doctor. Stopping a habit should not be your only health plan.

## Make the next look intentional

After an urge, your eyes want a replacement image. Give them a chosen one, not the next suggestion in a feed. A face you actually love. A task in front of you. The outdoors.

If you are a Christian, a psalm you already know is a fair place to put your mind. Say it slowly. Not as a magic spell. As a place to stand.

Do this especially in the hour before bed. That hour is training, whether you meant it or not. [Why you keep going back](/tracks/break-free/why-you-keep-going-back-to-porn) named the reward. This lesson names the daily material that reward has to compete with.

## Stay in motion

There is no certificate at the end of this track. There is a life that keeps the plan. Friction on the path, a ten-minute script, one witness, a morning-after truth, and attention pointed at something that can bear weight.

Go back to [lesson one](/tracks/break-free/how-to-stop-watching-porn) if the path got easy again. Look at the [Know Who You Are](/tracks/know-who-you-are) course when it opens if you need language for who you are when the urge argues. Until then, keep the sentence you can prove.

## Seven days of attention

Do not add all of this on top of some heroic overhaul. One block a day, twenty minutes, no feed. That is all.

1. Walk without a podcast. Notice the street. If your mind goes to the habit, label it "urge" and keep walking.
2. Read four pages of a paper book or a paper Bible. Stop at four if that is all you can stand. Staying is the rep.
3. Do one chore to the end. Dishes dried, floor swept, car cleaned out. Completion teaches your attention to finish.
4. Call or sit with a person and ask one question about their day. Listen past the first answer.
5. Work on one task with the phone in another room. When you want to check, stand up instead.
6. Eat one meal without a screen. Taste is slower than a clip. That is the point.
7. Review the week in ten lines. Where did the cue show up? Did the block happen? What will you repeat next week?

None of this fixes a brain by chemistry you can measure at home. What it does is simpler. It gives the old reward less rehearsal and gives a slower life more of it.

Keep these when the [30-day reset](/tracks/30-day-reset) opens. Keep them if it never opens. The practice is the reset.

## What you should not expect

Do not expect the pull to go silent because you had a good week. Do not expect a mood of purity to arrive and stay. Do not buy a program because it quotes a brain scan you cannot check.

If a change is real, you will notice it in small things. Duller interest in the old tabs. Shorter urges. A faster return to the script when the cue hits. That is enough.

If nothing changes after a steady month of the blocks, the friction, and the witness, get a counselor. Steadiness without any shift is a reason to get more help. It is not a reason to declare yourself the exception who needs the habit.

Keep the twenty-minute block next week. Same time if you can. The reset is boring on purpose. Haha. Boring is exactly what the old reward cannot stand.

A reset is not a feeling of being new. It is the old cue showing up and finding you already standing somewhere else.
