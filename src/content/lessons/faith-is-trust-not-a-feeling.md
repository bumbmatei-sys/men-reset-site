In the last lesson you saw that grace is a gift, and faith is the open hand that receives it. If you missed it, start with [What Grace Actually Is](/tracks/faith-grace-and-the-kingdom/what-grace-actually-is).

Now a question many men carry quietly. What if I do not feel any faith? What if I pray and feel nothing, read the Bible and feel nothing, sit in church and feel far away? Does that mean I do not have faith?

## What does faith really mean?

"Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen" (Hebrews 11:1).

Put simply, faith is trust. It is belief, confidence, and reliance on God. It is leaning your full weight on someone you cannot see, because of who He is and what He has said.

That is the key. Faith is anchored in God's character and God's word, not in an outcome you are hoping for and not in a feeling you managed to work up. A man can have strong faith on a day when he feels flat, because what he trusts has not changed.

Think of a small boy jumping off a ledge into his father's arms. He is not calm because he feels brave. He jumps because he knows his father. His trust is in the person, not the feeling.

## Is faith a feeling?

No. Feelings are real, and God cares about them, but they are not the foundation.

Feelings change with sleep, food, stress, weather, and a hundred other things. If your faith rises and falls with them, it will feel like a roller coaster. Faith is steadier because it looks outside you.

David wrote, "Whenever I am afraid, I will trust in You" (Psalm 56:3). He did not say, "Whenever I stop being afraid." He was afraid and trusting at the same time. That is what faith often looks like. Not the absence of fear, but a decision about where to lean.

If you have been trying to feel God's love and cannot, [Why You Can't Feel It](/tracks/know-gods-love/why-you-cant-feel-it) looks at what can block that. This lesson is about something different: trusting Him either way.

## Can faith and doubt coexist?

Yes. A father once brought his suffering son to Jesus and said, "Lord, I believe; help my unbelief!" (Mark 9:24). Belief and unbelief in one sentence. Jesus did not send him away to come back when his faith was purer. He healed the boy.

The disciples woke Jesus in a storm, terrified. He calmed the sea, then asked, "Why are you so fearful? How is it that you have no faith?" (Mark 4:40). It was a correction, but notice where it happened. In the boat, with Him, after He had already saved them.

So do not wait until your doubt is gone. Bring the faith you have to God, even if it feels like very little. Jesus said even faith "as a mustard seed" is enough to start with (Matthew 17:20). The size of your faith matters less than the One you put it in.

## How do you have faith in God?

You do not create faith out of nothing. Paul said God has given each believer "a measure of faith" (Romans 12:3). Your part is to feed it and use it. Here is how it grows.

1. **Hear God's word.** "So then faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God" (Romans 10:17). Read a little every day, even when it feels dry. If that is a struggle, [How to Read the Bible When You Don't Feel Like It](/teachings/read-the-bible-when-you-dont-feel-like-it) can help.
2. **Act on what you read.** "Be doers of the word, and not hearers only" (James 1:22). Faith that is used gets stronger.
3. **Say yes to the small things.** When you sense God asking for something simple, like an apology, a phone call, or turning something off, do it. Each yes builds trust.
4. **Pray honestly.** Tell Him what is real. If you keep falling and prayer feels hard, read [How to Pray When You Keep Falling](/teachings/how-to-pray-when-you-keep-falling).
5. **Thank Him for what He has already done.** Remembering His faithfulness is fuel for trusting Him now.

One more thing. Faith is not a way to talk God into something He was not willing to do. It lines your will up with His. "He who comes to God must believe that He is, and that He is a rewarder of those who diligently seek Him" (Hebrews 11:6). You are not convincing a reluctant God. You are coming to a Father who wants to be found.

## How do you have faith when everything is going wrong?

Look at Abraham. He waited years for a promise that looked impossible. "He did not waver at the promise of God through unbelief, but was strengthened in faith, giving glory to God, and being fully convinced that what He had promised He was also able to perform" (Romans 4:20-21).

He was not convinced by his circumstances. He was convinced by the One who promised. In a hard season, go back to what God has said, not to what you can see.

Hard seasons are also when you should not carry things alone. If you have felt low for weeks, cannot sleep, or have lost interest in nearly everything, talk to a doctor or a counselor as well as your pastor. That is wisdom, not weak faith. If you ever feel you might harm yourself, tell someone and get help right away, or get emergency help if you are in danger now.
