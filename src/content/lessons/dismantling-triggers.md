You already belong to Jesus. That does not change when a trigger shows up. Basically, the next seven days are about the doors you keep leaving open. The habit can be a screen, a drink, a substance, a game, or the old loop you already know. So you do not fight it by grit. You shut the door, and you stay near God.

Start with [the clean slate](/tracks/break-free-from-porn/identity-and-a-clean-slate) if you skipped days 1 to 7.

## Day 8. Soul fatigue and boundaries

Zechariah 4:6. "Not by might nor by power, but by My Spirit," says the Lord of hosts.

You are honest about your limits. You honor God by guarding against burnout. A drained day is when the habit sounds reasonable.

**Declaration.** I am honest about my limits. I honor God by guarding against burnout.

**Do this.** Do a soul-fatigue check. Find one ministry duty, work task, or social plan that is draining you. Say a clear no to it today.

**Prayer.** Holy Spirit, forgive me for working in my own strength. Refresh my tired soul and teach me the unforced rhythms of grace. Amen.

## Day 9. Clean up your digital space

Proverbs 16:6. By the fear of the Lord one departs from evil.

Your eyes and your mind belong to God. You shut every digital door to secret compromise.

**Declaration.** My eyes and my mind belong to God. I shut every digital door to secret compromise.

**Do this.**

- Install strong content blockers.
- Remove the apps that trigger you.
- Set one rule you do not negotiate: **no phone, tablet, or laptop in the bedroom overnight.**

**Prayer.** Lord, I give You my devices today. Let what I do online match who I am in Jesus. Amen.

## Day 10. A quiet day

Psalm 119:11. Your word I have hidden in my heart, that I might not sin against You.

Your desires answer to the Holy Spirit. Cheap hits have no power over your appetites. This is not a science lecture. It is one quiet day.

**Declaration.** My desires answer to the Holy Spirit. Cheap hits have no power over my appetites.

**Do this.** Do a full 24-hour media fast. No TV, no scrolling, no streaming, no video games. Let your mind reset in quiet time with God.

**Prayer.** Father, I put my appetites under You today. Quiet the noise so I can hear Your still, small voice again. Amen.

## Day 11. Back to your first love

Revelation 2:4-5. You have left your first love. Remember therefore from where you have fallen. Repent and do the first works.

You reject autopilot faith. Jesus is your first love and your delight.

**Declaration.** I reject autopilot faith. Jesus is my first love and my delight.

**Do this.** Remember the joy you had when you first met Jesus. Spend one full hour today in private worship, praise, and thanks. Do not ask Him for a single thing.

**Prayer.** Lord Jesus, forgive me for coasting on past victories. Light the fire of first love in me again today. Amen.

## Day 12. The fear of the Lord

Proverbs 9:10. The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom, and the knowledge of the Holy One is understanding.

You walk in the fear of the Lord. You value His presence far above any secret, short pleasure.

**Declaration.** I walk in the fear of the Lord. I value His presence far above any secret, short pleasure.

**Do this.** Practice God's presence. Set an alarm for every two hours. When it rings, stop, take a deep breath, and remember that God is in the room with you.

**Prayer.** Holy God, remove every hard layer of compromise in my heart. Give me back a deep awe for Your name. Amen.

## Day 13. Delight in the Word

Psalm 119:103. How sweet are Your words to my taste, sweeter than honey to my mouth.

God's Word satisfies you more than any screen.

**Declaration.** God's Word satisfies me more than any screen.

**Do this.** Swap 45 minutes of screen time for reading three chapters of the Gospel of John out loud. Go slowly and let every word sink in.

**Prayer.** Lord, open my eyes to the beauty of Your Word. Let Your truth fill my hunger today. Amen.

## Day 14. The end in view

Psalm 37:37. Mark the perfect man and behold the upright, for the end (akarit) of that man is peace.

Akarit is the end. The outcome. Peace. You live with that end in view. You choose lasting peace over five seconds of impulse.

**Declaration.** I live with my akarit in view. I choose lasting peace over five seconds of impulse.

**Do this.** Write your spiritual obituary on an index card. How do you want your life, your legacy, and your faith to be remembered when you stand before Jesus? Keep the card in your wallet.

**Prayer.** Father, keep eternity in front of my eyes today. Let my final destination shape every private choice. Amen.
