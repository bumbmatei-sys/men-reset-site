You know God loves you. You have heard it a hundred times. You might even teach it. But when you sit down to pray, it feels like talking to the ceiling.

That gap between knowing and feeling is common. It does not make you a bad Christian. But it is worth asking why, because God never meant His love to stay a fact in your head.

## The hose is not broken

Think about a garden hose. The water is on. The source is full. But if something is blocking it along the line, only a trickle comes out, or nothing at all.

God's love works the same way. The source never runs dry. He does not love you less on the days you feel nothing. But things on our side can pinch the flow until we stop sensing it.

So the question is not "Why did God stop?" He didn't. The better question is "Where is the blockage?" Here are six of the most common.

## 1. Fear

Fear says, "If I get close, I will get hurt." Maybe you were let down by people who were supposed to love you. Maybe you still picture God as angry. Either way, part of you keeps Him at arm's length to stay safe.

1 John 4:18 says there is no fear in love, and that perfect love casts out fear. Notice the order. You do not get rid of the fear so that love can come in. Love comes in and pushes the fear out. You just have to stop guarding the door.

## 2. Independence

Some of us are proud of not needing anyone. We handle things. We don't ask for help. It has served us well at work and maybe in life.

But love is received by people who know they need it. A child does not earn a hug. He runs and falls into it. Independence keeps you standing with your arms crossed while God stands with His open. Jesus said unless you become like little children, you will not enter the kingdom (Matthew 18:3). Dependence is not weakness with God. It is the way in.

## 3. Offense

Offense is a quiet one. Maybe you prayed for something and it did not happen. Someone died. A marriage ended. A door closed. Somewhere inside, you decided God let you down.

You can still go to church with that. You can still serve. But you will not feel close to someone you are holding a grudge against. The fix is honesty. Tell Him. The Psalms are full of people saying, "Why, Lord?" He can handle it. What blocks the flow is not the question. It is the silence where the question should be.

## 4. Shame

Guilt says, "I did something wrong." Shame says, "I am something wrong." And shame makes you hide, like Adam and Eve in the trees.

If deep down you believe you are the exception to His love, you will block it every time it comes near. You will think, "That is for other people." Read [Shame vs Guilt](/teachings/shame-vs-guilt-jesus-2026) if this one is yours. Jesus did not only pay for what you did. He came for who you are.

## 5. Performance

This one looks good from the outside. You pray, read, serve, give. But underneath, you are doing it to stay loved. So you never rest, because you are never sure you have done enough.

You cannot feel a gift you are still trying to pay for. Love that has to be earned is a wage, and wages never feel like love. Your identity is not what you do. It is whose you are. [Identity in Christ](/teachings/identity-in-christ-2026) goes deeper on that.

## 6. Full hands

You cannot receive something with your hands full. Some of us are holding so much that there is no room: worry, plans, screens, other comforts we reach for first. When we are empty and lonely, we grab something quick instead of waiting on Him.

It is not that those things are all bad. It is that they fill the space where His love was supposed to go. Receiving means putting something down.

## Hurry is an enemy of love

Running through all six is one thing that makes every blockage worse: hurry.

Love takes time. You cannot rush a meal with someone you love and call it connection. You cannot read a verse in thirty seconds between notifications and expect to feel known. Love needs attention, and hurry steals attention.

Jesus was never in a hurry. People interrupted Him all the time, and He stopped. Martha was busy serving, and Mary sat at His feet. Jesus said Mary chose the better part (Luke 10:42).

If you want to feel His love, slow down. Not forever. Just long enough to notice Him.

## A word on dry seasons

Sometimes you clear every blockage you can find and it still feels dry. That happens. Faithful people in the Bible had long seasons of feeling far away. It does not mean He left.

And sometimes the numbness is not spiritual at all. Grief, exhaustion, or depression can flatten every feeling, including this one. If that is you, talking to a pastor or a good counselor is not a lack of faith. God often heals through people.

## Do this today

Look back at the six blockages. Pick the one that hit closest.

Each day this week, take five unhurried minutes. Put your phone in another room. Name that blockage to God out loud. Then ask Him, "What do You want to say to me about this?" Sit still and listen. Write down whatever comes, even if it is small.

## Reflect

Which blockage do you recognize most in yourself, and when did it start?
