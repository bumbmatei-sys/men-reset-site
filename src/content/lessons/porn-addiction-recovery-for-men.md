**Stopping a habit** fails when it is only a ban. You remove the thing and leave a hole. The hole fills with the same night, because nothing else has a claim on it.

This lesson is about who you practice being on a normal Wednesday. The idea is simple. You become what you repeatedly do, so aim the repetition on purpose. The steps here are written for this fight.

And first, grace. Jesus is not waiting for you to have a clean week before He is close to you. He is close now. That is exactly why we can look at this honestly and not fall apart.

## Stop living as a restart

A lot of us run the same loop. Slip. Hate it. Promise a new era on Monday. Hold on by your teeth until Thursday. Slip. The era never gets a full week, so it never becomes a life.

Basically, recovery is a week that looks like the person you meant. Not a highlight. A week. Meals, work, a conversation, sleep, and a plan for the hour when you usually disappear. If you only gear up after a failure, you are training the failure to be the start of your effort.

## Practice a sentence you can prove

Pick a sentence that would still be true if someone checked.

"I am a person who leaves my phone in the kitchen at night."

"I am a person who tells the truth the next morning."

"I am a person who does not scroll in the bathroom."

Do not pick "I am free" if you cannot show it. Pick a behavior. Do it today. The Christian frame for this is renewal of the mind, not a costume. You are not faking a testimony. You are giving your attention a different job, so the old pattern has less room.

## Guard the hour you come home

For many of us the danger is not the office. It is the decompression. You walk in, you want to be done, and the fastest done is a screen. The habit you are fighting is often the last stop on a road that started with a feed, a game, or a drink.

Decide the first twenty minutes at home before you are in them. Shoes off, a real greeting if someone is there, food, a shower. Phone stays in a pocket or a bowl. You are not becoming a monk. You are refusing to hand the transition to a habit that does not care about your house.

Sleep is part of the same guard. A wrecked night makes the next evening dumber. If you can move bedtime earlier by thirty minutes, do that before you add a complicated routine. The [morning track](/tracks/discipline-and-morning-routine) will go further. It is not required to start.

## Get one honest friend

A streak app can lie, and it can become another private score. A person cannot be minimized as easily. You do not need a group of ten. You need one person. A brother, a friend, or your spouse if that is safe and she or he has agreed to hear it. Someone who knows the cue and the plan.

Report the behavior, not a performance. "Phone was in the kitchen. I wanted to bail at 10:40 and I did the walk instead." If you slipped, say that in the same report. Recovery that only announces clean days is still hiding.

If you have no one, say so in the reflection and make finding that person the action for the week. Do not pretend a notes app is the same thing forever. It can hold you for a few days.

## Count the honest days

Abstinence matters. It is not the only number. Count days you told the truth, days you left the room, days you did the first-hour plan. Those are the days the new default is being built. A clean day you spent numb and alone is thinner than a day you slipped and then told someone before noon.

I want to be clear here. I am not saying staying clean does not matter. What I am saying is that honesty is part of the same fight.

## An ordinary week you can copy

Here is a shape, not a law. Change the clock to match your job.

- Wake and stand up before you touch a screen. The [morning track](/tracks/discipline-and-morning-routine) will expand this. For now, feet on the floor is the win.
- Work block with the phone in another room if your job allows it. If it does not, the browser rule still stands.
- The first twenty minutes at home, as written above, before any "decompression" scroll.
- A hard stop for games and feeds. Pick a time you can keep on a weekday, even if the weekend is still messy.
- Phone in the kitchen before you brush your teeth.
- One true report to your friend, including a slip if there was one.

Miss a piece and do the next piece. Recovery is not a chain that snaps. It is a set of moves. The person you are becoming is the one who returns to the moves. That is the same idea as the identity essay linked below. You vote for an identity with a behavior, not with a caption.

## When the week feels pointless

Around day four the new moves feel fake. You are doing them and you do not feel like a different person. That is normal. Identity lags behind the behavior. Keep the kitchen charger and the twenty-minute arrival home even when they feel like a costume. The costume is how the next month gets a shape.

If you miss two days, do not write a story about your type. Write the cue that knocked you off and put the next move back on the calendar for tonight. We stall here because we want the feeling of recovery before we have the week. Take the week first.

Read [how to quit porn and masturbation](/tracks/break-free/how-to-quit-porn-and-masturbation) next if those two habits are tied together for you. Separating them on paper while they travel as a pair will just give you a loophole.

Trust Him with this week. One honest day, then the next one.
