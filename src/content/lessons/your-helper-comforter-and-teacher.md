Many of us treat the Holy Spirit like a fire extinguisher behind glass. Good to know He is there. Break the glass in an emergency. Otherwise, leave Him on the wall.

Jesus had something very different in mind.

## Where we are in this course

In lesson 1 you saw [who the Holy Spirit is](/tracks/walking-with-the-holy-spirit/who-the-holy-spirit-is): a Person, fully God, who lives in every believer. This lesson is about what He does. Most of it comes straight from Jesus' own words on His last night with the disciples, in John 14 to 16.

## What does the Holy Spirit do?

Jesus did not give the disciples a list of rules for when He was gone. He gave them a Person. "And I will pray the Father, and He will give you another Helper, that He may abide with you forever" (John 14:16).

The Greek word is parakletos. It means someone called alongside to help. A helper, a comforter, an advocate, a counselor. Notice the word "another." Jesus had been their Helper for three years. Now they would have another one just like Him, who would never leave.

Here is what Jesus and the apostles say He does.

## He helps you

The first name Jesus gives Him is Helper. That means you are not meant to live the Christian life by willpower alone.

Paul wrote that the Spirit "helps in our weaknesses" (Romans 8:26). Not only in your strengths. In the weak places: the temper you cannot control, the worry that will not leave, the habit that keeps coming back, the conversation you dread.

Help usually comes quietly. A sentence you did not plan to say. The strength to stay calm. A sudden reminder to stop and pray. A desire to do the right thing that was not there yesterday. Paul put it this way: "it is God who works in you both to will and to do for His good pleasure" (Philippians 2:13).

## He teaches you and reminds you

"He will teach you all things, and bring to your remembrance all things that I said to you" (John 14:26).

The Spirit inspired the Scriptures, and He is the best teacher of them. When you read the Bible with Him, the words stop being only information. A verse you have read many times suddenly speaks to your situation. That is Him.

He also reminds. You may have read a verse months ago, and in a hard moment it comes back to you. You did not search for it. It came. That is part of His work too. So feed Him something to remind you of. The more of the Word you take in, the more He has to bring back. If reading feels dry, [How to Read the Bible When You Don't Feel Like It](/teachings/read-the-bible-when-you-dont-feel-like-it) has simple steps.

## He guides you into truth

"However, when He, the Spirit of truth, has come, He will guide you into all truth" (John 16:13).

He guides like someone walking ahead of you on a path you do not know. Not usually with a full map, but one step at a time. He will never guide you against what He already said in Scripture. And everything He shows you leads back to Jesus: "He will glorify Me, for He will take of what is Mine and declare it to you" (John 16:14). Lesson 4 looks at how to recognize His leading and test it.

## He comforts you

Older Bible translations call Him the Comforter. Jesus said, "I will not leave you orphans; I will come to you" (John 14:18). The Spirit is the answer to the loneliness of a believer who feels he has to face everything by himself.

His comfort is not a pat on the head that ignores the pain. It is the presence of God in the pain. He brings peace that does not depend on how things look. Many people describe it as a weight lifting, or a calm they cannot explain.

## He helps you pray

"For we do not know what we should pray for as we ought, but the Spirit Himself makes intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered" (Romans 8:26).

There are days when you have no words. Grief, confusion, exhaustion. On those days, prayer does not depend on you finding the right sentences. The Spirit prays in you and for you. Sometimes the best prayer is simply, "Holy Spirit, I don't know what to say. Pray through me." If prayer has been hard lately, read [How to Pray When You Keep Falling](/teachings/how-to-pray-when-you-keep-falling).

## He tells you whose you are

"The Spirit Himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God" (Romans 8:16). And Paul says "the love of God has been poured out in our hearts by the Holy Spirit who was given to us" (Romans 5:5).

This may be His most important work in you. He does not only tell you that God loves people in general. He tells your heart that the heavenly Father loves you, and that you belong in His family. If that still feels far away, read [What Does It Mean to Be a Child of God?](/teachings/what-does-it-mean-to-be-a-child-of-god).

## He gives you power

"But you shall receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you; and you shall be witnesses to Me" (Acts 1:8).

The power of the Spirit is not for showing off. It is for living like Jesus and telling others about Him. Some of the boldest people in the Bible were ordinary fishermen. What changed them was not training. It was the Spirit.

## Is the Holy Spirit our helper only in emergencies?

No. Jesus said He would "abide with you forever." Abide means live with, stay with. He is with you in the boring hours as much as the crisis. Most of walking with Him happens on normal days, in small choices. That is what the next lesson is about.
