**How to not relapse** is a different question from how to quit. Quitting has energy. You are angry at the habit, you have a plan, and every clean day feels like a win. Then the weeks turn into months. Nobody is cheering anymore. Life gets ordinary, and the old habit starts to look interesting again.

This course is for that stretch. It is for men and women who have stopped porn, gaming, the late scroll, or another loop, and now want to stay stopped. Six short lessons, one step each. Why the later months can feel harder than the first week. The quiet drift that comes before most falls. What to do in the hour of a close call. An ordinary week that holds. What to do with boredom and the flat days. And how to get up the same day if you do fall.

You are not staying free by willpower alone. Jesus set you free, and your heavenly Father keeps walking with you on the plain days, not only the dramatic ones. Your part is to keep showing up.

If you are still in the first days, start with [Break Free](/tracks/break-free) or the [30-Day Reset](/tracks/30-day-reset). This course picks up after that, for the long run.
