Paul said it short. "Be on the alert, stand firm in the faith, act like men, be strong. Let all that you do be done in love." (1 Corinthians 16:13-14)

I love this verse because the last line changes everything. Strength without love is not strength.

Before we start, let me say this. This is not about shaming you. I am not here to tell you that you are weak. If you are tired, if you messed up, grace is where we start. Jesus already did the work. You are not reading this to earn anything. You are reading this to remember who you are.

Basically, a strong person is not the loud one. It is the one who stays when it gets hard, and loves when it would be easier to close off. Some steps here talk about loving your wife. If you are a woman, the same heart works the other way around. So this is for you too.
