A **morning routine for discipline** fails when it needs a perfect life. You do not need an ice bath or a 5 a.m. alarm. You need a start that happens before the phone gets a vote.

This course is for anyone with a job, a family, a commute or a shift. Men and women. Six short lessons, one small move each. Wake at a time you can keep. Let the phone sleep in another room. Do one chore before you sit. Spend a few minutes with your heavenly Father in His word. Leave the house without a scroll. And when the night went badly, start the morning anyway.

Discipline here is not about earning anything. Jesus already did the work. You are learning to order your morning around what is true, one small step at a time.

If you want the whole routine on one page first, read [A Christian Morning Routine for Men With a Job](/teachings/christian-morning-routine-men-2026). This course takes it apart and builds it slowly, one piece a lesson.

If nights are the real leak, start with the open course: [how to stop watching porn](/tracks/break-free/how-to-stop-watching-porn). A morning routine on top of a wrecked night will not hold for long.
