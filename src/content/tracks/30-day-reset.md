Basically, a **30-day reset** is thirty ordinary days with Jesus in front of the habit. So you do not start by making a vow you cannot keep. You start from the bath He already gave you.

The habit can be porn, a game, the phone, a drink, a substance, or another loop. Men and women. Same standing. Same next step.

## What you can read here

Days 1 to 7 are open. That phase is [identity and a clean slate](/tracks/break-free-from-porn/identity-and-a-clean-slate). You are not earning a clean record. Jesus already bathed you. The week is foot washing.

## The rest of the month

Days 8 to 30 stay in the plan. Open a phase and you will see a short note, then a way to get the full month by email.

- Days 8 to 14: [dismantling triggers](/tracks/break-free-from-porn/dismantling-triggers).
- Days 15 to 21: [activating faith](/tracks/break-free-from-porn/activating-faith).
- Days 22 to 30: [lasting freedom](/tracks/break-free-from-porn/lasting-freedom).
