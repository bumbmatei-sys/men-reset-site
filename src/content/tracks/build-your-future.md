**Build Your Future** is for anyone who looks back and sees wasted years, or looks ahead and sees fog. Men and women.

God does not waste what you hand Him. So we start with your past, then your story, then fear, then the next step, and we finish by writing the vision down.

## The 6 lessons

1. [He Wastes Nothing](/tracks/build-your-future/he-wastes-nothing)  
   What Romans 8:28 really promises.
2. [He Redeems the Lows](/tracks/build-your-future/he-redeems-the-lows)  
   No place is too low for Him.
3. [Believe Your Story](/tracks/build-your-future/believe-your-story)  
   What Jesus did in what happened to you.
4. [The Fear Journey](/tracks/build-your-future/fear-journey)  
   How fear actually leaves.
5. [Destiny Is the Next Step](/tracks/build-your-future/destiny-is-the-step)  
   God lights one step at a time.
6. [Write the Vision](/tracks/build-your-future/write-the-vision)  
   Make it plain, then walk it out.
