**Know His Love** is for anyone who is not sure God is for them. Men and women.

Most of us try to earn love. We perform, then we wait to see if it worked. But God loved you first. Jesus went to the cross before you changed anything. So this course starts there.

We look at why He loves you, what His heart has always been like, what blocks you from feeling it, and what to do when you feel far. Then we finish with how His love flows through you to other people.

## The 6 lessons

1. [Loved Before You Do Anything](/tracks/know-gods-love/loved-before-you-do-anything)  
   He proved His love before you changed a thing.
2. [His Heart Has Not Changed](/tracks/know-gods-love/his-heart-has-not-changed)  
   From Eden to the cross, His heart is mercy.
3. [Why You Can't Feel It](/tracks/know-gods-love/why-you-cant-feel-it)  
   The blockages that pinch the flow.
4. [The One Jesus Loves](/tracks/know-gods-love/the-one-jesus-loves)  
   John believed it, and it changed him.
5. [When You Feel Far](/tracks/know-gods-love/when-you-feel-far)  
   Three questions and a fresh start.
6. [Love That Flows Through You](/tracks/know-gods-love/love-that-flows-through-you)  
   Receive it, then give it away with no hook.
