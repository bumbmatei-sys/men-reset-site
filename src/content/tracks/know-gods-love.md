**Know His Love** is for anyone who is not sure God is for them. Men and women.

Most of us try to earn love. We perform, then we wait to see if it worked. But God loved you first. Jesus went to the cross before you changed anything. So this course starts there.

We look at why He loves you, what His heart has always been like, what blocks you from feeling it, and what to do when you feel far. Then we finish with how His love flows through you to other people.
