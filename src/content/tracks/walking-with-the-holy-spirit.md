**Walking with the Holy Spirit** is not only for preachers or for people who grew up in a certain kind of church. Jesus promised Him to every believer. "And I will pray the Father, and He will give you another Helper, that He may abide with you forever" (John 14:16).

Many Christians know a lot about the heavenly Father and about Jesus, but the Holy Spirit stays vague. A wind, a feeling, a doctrine on a page. This course is here to change that. It is for men and women, new believers and people who have been in church for years.

Six short lessons, one step each. You will learn who the Holy Spirit is, and why He is a Person and not a force. What He does in your life as Helper, Comforter, and Teacher. What fellowship with Him looks like on an ordinary day. How He speaks and how to test what you hear. How His conviction is different from condemnation. And how to keep being filled and walk in step with Him, so His fruit grows in you.

This is about friendship, not performance. You do not have to earn His presence. If you belong to Jesus, He already lives in you. The goal is to know Him.

If you are not sure yet where you stand with God, start with [Know His Love](/tracks/know-gods-love). For a short overview before you begin, read [How to Build a Relationship With the Holy Spirit](/teachings/relationship-with-the-holy-spirit).
