**How to quit gaming addiction** and how to put the phone down are the same kind of problem for a lot of people. The session was going to be twenty minutes. It became the night. Work, sleep, and the people in the house got whatever was left.

This course is for men and women who keep losing evenings they meant to keep. Six short lessons, one move each. See where the night goes. Set a hard stop before you start. Leave the room and use your hands in the first ten minutes after you close the game. Set up your phone in daylight so it holds at 11 p.m. Fill the empty evening on purpose. And when you break the rule, come back the same hour.

Games are not the enemy, and neither is your phone. The goal is evenings that belong to what matters most: your sleep, your work, your people, and time with your heavenly Father. You do not do this to earn His love. You already have it. You do it to make room for Him.

If you want the bigger questions first, like whether gaming is a sin or whether to quit all at once or slowly, read [How to Quit Gaming as a Christian](/teachings/quit-gaming-christian-2026). If the late scroll in bed is the main leak, read [How to Stop Scrolling at Night](/teachings/stop-scrolling-at-night-christian).

The urge plan from [Break Free](/tracks/break-free) carries over here too. Name the cue, leave the room, and do one physical thing before you negotiate with yourself.
