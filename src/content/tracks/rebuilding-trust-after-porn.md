**How to rebuild trust after porn** is one of the hardest questions a married man can ask. You have told her. Maybe it came out all at once, maybe in pieces. Now the house is quiet in a new way, and you do not know what to do with your hands.

This course is for the husband after the confession. It is not about how to tell her. If you have not done that yet, start with [How to Tell Your Wife About Porn](/teachings/how-to-tell-your-wife-about-porn). And it is not an argument about whether porn counts as betrayal. If you need that settled first, read [Is Watching Porn Cheating?](/teachings/is-watching-porn-cheating)

Six short lessons, one step each. What the first days ask of you. Why a steady pattern speaks louder than a promise. How to listen when she is still hurting. How to stay close when you want to pull away. Where to take your own shame, so she does not have to carry it. And what a long faithfulness looks like, month after month.

This course is written for you, the husband, with deep respect for your wife. Her pain is real, and it is not a problem to manage. Her healing has its own pace. Forgiveness is hers to give, not something you are owed. Your part is to become a man she can trust again, whether or not she is ready to say so.

If there is any danger or harm in your home right now, safety comes first. Lessons 1 and 3 explain where to get help. A good Christian counselor, ideally one experienced with couples and betrayal, is wisdom, not weak faith.

You do not do this alone. Your heavenly Father is not finished with you or with your marriage. If you are not sure He still loves you after what you did, read [Does God Still Love Me After Porn?](/teachings/does-god-still-love-me-after-porn) first. Then come back and take the first step.
