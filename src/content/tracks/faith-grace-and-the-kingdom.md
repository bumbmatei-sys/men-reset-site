**Grace and the kingdom of God** get used as an excuse or as a threat. Neither helps anyone who wants to stop. Grace is not permission to keep the tab open. The kingdom is not a scoreboard that deletes you after a bad Friday.

This track is coming soon. It will stay practical: what confession looks like with one trusted person, what you do the morning after, and how to pray when you do not feel worthy of the sentence.

Start with [identity and a clean slate](/tracks/break-free/identity-and-a-clean-slate). Shame tells you to hide. Hiding is how the habit gets the next night. Jesus already bathed you. The days after that are smaller than a public speech.
