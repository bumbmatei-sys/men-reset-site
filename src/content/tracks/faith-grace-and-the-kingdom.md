**What it means to be a citizen of the kingdom of God** is not a question for seminary students only. It is a question for every man who wants to know where he stands with God and how to live on a Monday morning.

This course is for men and women. Six short lessons, one step each. You will learn what grace actually is, and what it is not. How faith works when you do not feel it. What Jesus meant when He said the Kingdom had come near. How faith and works fit together without turning the Christian life back into a wage. What grace does when you fall. And a plain daily and weekly way of life for a citizen of the Kingdom.

None of this is about earning your heavenly Father's love. You already have it. This is about learning to live like a son in the King's house.

If you are not yet sure that God is for you, start with [Know His Love](/tracks/know-gods-love). If you want to settle who you are in Christ, take [Know Who You Are](/tracks/know-who-you-are). For the short version on grace first, read [What Is Grace in the Bible?](/teachings/what-is-grace-2026)
