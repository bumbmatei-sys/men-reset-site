**How to renew your mind** is not a mystery reserved for a few strong Christians. It is a daily practice. You notice what you are thinking, you bring it to your heavenly Father, and you agree with what He says instead of what the old thought says.

This course is for men and women. Six short lessons, one new step each. You will learn why a bad thought is not yet a sin, what Romans 12:2 actually asks of you, how to tell the voice of accusation from the voice of conviction, how to catch a thought in the first few seconds, how to answer it with one verse said out loud, and what to do when the same thought keeps coming back.

You are not doing this by willpower. The Holy Spirit renews you. Your part is to keep showing up and keep agreeing with Him.

If you want the short version on one page first, read [How to Take Every Thought Captive](/teachings/take-every-thought-captive-2026). This course goes slower and deeper, one piece at a time.

If you do not yet believe your heavenly Father is for you, start with [Know His Love](/tracks/know-gods-love). A renewed mind grows best in a heart that knows it is loved.
