**Identity in Christ for men** is not a slogan you stick over a double life. It is the claim that your name is not “the guy who always goes back.” The urge will argue. A coming lesson will walk through what you say back, in plain words, without pretending the habit is gone.

This track is coming soon. While you wait, read [porn addiction recovery for men](/tracks/break-free-from-porn/porn-addiction-recovery-for-men). That lesson treats identity as a practice: you rehearse the man you are becoming on the days you do not feel like him.

If you are not a Christian, you can still use the practical half. Pick a sentence that is true and specific, such as “I do not open that site on a weeknight.” Say it before the urge, not after the shame.
