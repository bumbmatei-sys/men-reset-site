**How to deal with loneliness as a Christian** is a question many men carry quietly. You can have a job, a church, even a family, and still have nobody who knows how your week really went. Loneliness is not a sin, and it is not a punishment. It is a real need your heavenly Father made you with, and He has an answer for it.

This course is written for the men on this site, and it is open to anyone who feels alone. Six short lessons, one step each. Name the loneliness honestly and remember you are a son, not an orphan. Find one friend. Learn to be a friend yourself. Move from sitting in a church seat to belonging. Start a weekly table that fills the loneliest evening of your week. And keep going on the days you still feel alone.

You will not build a new life in a week. You do not need to. You need one small step, repeated, and a Father who goes with you.

If loneliness is the moment temptation shows up for you, start with [Loneliness and Temptation](/teachings/loneliness-and-temptation). It gives you a plan for the lonely hour. This course goes after the life around that hour.
