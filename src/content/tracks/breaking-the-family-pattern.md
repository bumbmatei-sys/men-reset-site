**How to break generational patterns** is a question many men carry without saying it out loud. Maybe you grew up around addiction, anger, or a father who was not there. Maybe it was quieter than that: a house where nobody talked, or where love always came with a condition. Now you have a home of your own, or you will one day, and you do not want to pass it on.

This course is for you. Six short lessons, one step each. Name the pattern you grew up in, honestly and without blaming anyone for drama's sake. Decide what stops with you, and what you will give instead. Look at the father you had and the Father you have. Catch the old moves, anger, silence, or leaving, and practice a better one. Build a different home out of small daily things. And when you slip into the old way, repair it the same day and keep going.

You are not doomed to repeat your family's story. "If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; old things have passed away; behold, all things have become new" (2 Corinthians 5:17). Your heavenly Father is not finished with you, and He is not finished with your family.

This is pastoral teaching, not therapy. A counselor and a doctor are wisdom, not weak faith, and some lessons will tell you when to call one. If you want to start with who you are before you look at where you came from, read [Know Who You Are](/tracks/know-who-you-are).
